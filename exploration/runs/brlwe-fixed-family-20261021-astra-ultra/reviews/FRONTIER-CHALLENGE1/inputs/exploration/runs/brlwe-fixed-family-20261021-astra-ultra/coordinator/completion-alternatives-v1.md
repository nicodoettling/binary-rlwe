# FF2 completion alternatives, first constructive wave

Author investigation notes; no impossibility of the main objective is asserted.

## What the fixed-q certificate actually changes

CRT-PROJ-v1 and CERT-EXTRACT-v1 would replace the one-selected-value absolute guessing comparison by an identifying tuple at one fixed split modulus. Multiple distinct roots consume repeated prime factors through Smith normal form. This is a new argument, not deletion of DISTINCT primes or COMMON partition from DD's average-prime theorem. Their finite fixed-family corollary keeps q(n),sigma(n) fixed before k and epsilon. The whole-secret information bound can apply to a certificate through a mathematically defined exhaustive inverse; that inverse must never be charged as an efficient reduction step.

The separate A-FRAC completion still uses a common G in J and independently completed noise over a Kn-by-n stacked matrix. The guard sigma≥2ts sqrt(Kn)L cannot be inherited at fixed sigma for arbitrarily high polynomial K. The certificate increases K; it does not solve this edge.

## Alternate constructions and exact missing interfaces

1. **Shrink the continuous source width.** In the current B-GP map, s²=w²+r_B² and r_B≥sqrt(log(8n/epsilon_B)/pi). Shrinking w leaves a positive smoothing floor, so it does not give s=O(K^-1/2). A new narrow-source bridge would need effective weighted coset probabilities, both normalizers and exact uniform comparator. Ordinary HNF alone gives a fundamental domain with potentially exponentially small guaranteed inradius. Such a bridge is open, not excluded by the present sufficient smoothing condition.

2. **Reveal correlated completion noise.** Choosing H=-MG+E trivially gives exact E, but revealing H then changes G's posterior and can reveal the secret. FF1's finite witness has guessing probability3/4 from Y and1 from(Y,H). Any alternative must bound the actual normalized posterior without assuming G remains independent after revealing H. The covariance obstruction applies only to the stated independent/additive model; it is not a theorem about all nonlinear kernels.

3. **Integer linear sample generation.** For a conditional fixed weight matrix W mapping N independent scalar error blocks to k nonzero integer-weight outputs, ||W||op²≥||W||F²/N≥k/N. An independent Gaussian covariance complement therefore needs output/input variance ratio at least k/N. Ring block weights have the analogous trace/rank bound after charging n coordinates. This calculation only diagnoses that linear independent-noise construction. A computationally pseudorandom sample generator or secret-dependent nonlinear construction is a different obligation.

4. **Scale the fractional source lattice.** Replacing J^-1 by Q^-1 J^-1 also changes its integral dual ideal to QJ. Mask integrality then requires G∈QJ, so the old small-index entropy bound cannot be carried across unchanged: N(QJ)=Q^n N(J). A scalar reparametrization that changes only covariance and ignores this ideal/entropy change is invalid. A new source map with useful parameters could still be proved.

5. **Recover all roots after an identifying subset.** The partial uniform-noise endpoint is justified only for small retained sets H. Beyond that size, known secret values reveal E_H, and simulating the other error coordinates requires the exact conditional discrete Gaussian on the corresponding CRT-coset lattice. A marginal CRT Gaussian or independent coordinate resampling changes the joint error law. Efficient narrow conditional ideal sampling is an unproved additional interface; injectivity of the binary tuple gives no efficient inverse by itself.

6. **Rectangular/subring computational hybrid.** Micciancio's arbitrary-sample vector-LWE construction has a genuine lower-dimensional matrix hash and a sign diagonal. A direct rank-one port loses the negacyclic algebra under general coordinate signs, and a noiseless retained full-ring hash(a,aS) can be tested by dividing by a unit and checking binary coefficients. Support bounds alone would not rule out a hidden/noisy computational endpoint. A materially new construction must specify a ring-compatible rectangular or subfield map, the FULL target public/error law, and an independently justified pseudorandomness premise. Neither the vector theorem nor absence of a found attack supplies that bridge.

7. **Good-secret amplification.** Exact Galois affine symmetries preserve the binary cube and coefficient symmetric Gaussian errors, but they need not enlarge every orbit's intersection with a rare good-secret set. Fresh decision coins also do not change that measure. CERT-EXTRACT uses the same fixed good secret at every stage and retains its epsilon-scale probability; it claims no amplification to all secrets.

Next substantive tests: full probability/source review of the certificate; a quantitative average-cube Gaussian correction challenge (exact covariance failure on four rare secrets is insufficient for negligible-TV exclusion); a fully specified narrow-coset sampler or a counterexample to a proposed implementation; and a concrete computational rectangular-ring endpoint. These are open tasks, not an exhaustion certificate.
