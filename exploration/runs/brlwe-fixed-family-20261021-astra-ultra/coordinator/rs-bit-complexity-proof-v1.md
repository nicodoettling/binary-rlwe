# Proof of SV-RS-BIT-v1

The quotient exponent is the lcm of reduced denominators of the ordinary basis inverse, obtained with polynomial binary arithmetic. Under the stated promise it equals the odd prime p. Set m=ceil(log2 p). Since F_p^* is cyclic of order p-1 and 2n divides p-1, the homomorphism b -> b^((p-1)/(2n)) maps F_p^* onto the 2n-th roots with equal fibers. Exactly half these roots have exact order2n: because2n is a power of two, this is equivalent to alpha^n=-1. Thus exactly(p-1)/2 of the integers b in[1,p-1] succeed. For the m-bit proposal, the exact one-trial success probability is

    theta=(p-1)/2^(m+1) >=1/8.

Indeed2^m<2p and p>=3 give theta>(p-1)/(4p)>=1/6>1/8. The weaker1/8 bound is sufficient. Conditional on every sequence of earlier failures, fresh m fair bits give precisely the same theta. Hence the exact failure probability after L trials is(1-theta)^L, justified by this full-history conditional law. Since7^8=5764801<2^23=8388608, (7/8)^8<1/2. With a_int=ceil(log2(1/eta)), L=8a_int, failure probability is at most2^-a_int<=eta. There is no unsupported multiplication of posterior marginal bounds.

Every accepted alpha has exact order2n. The n odd powers enumerate all primitive roots, regardless of the distribution of the accepted alpha. For RS interval recognition or GAP optimization the same full set of roots is therefore searched. The source proof's basis evaluations use n roots and n basis columns, polynomial arithmetic and modular exponentiation in O(log p)-bit integers; computing powers uses O(log p) bit-length exponents. Berlekamp-Welch uses linear systems of size polynomial in n. Rational lifting and inverse-ideal membership use complete input bit lengths. The correct lambda is deterministic once any primitive root succeeds, under the corresponding source guard. The only new random failure is the explicit root-acquisition FAIL, and no incorrect output is introduced on promised inputs.

All random-bit sampling and trial loops are explicitly capped. Integer ceilings for m and a_int can be determined by bit lengths and exact comparisons on the supplied rational eta; no transcendental evaluation is required. The whole schedule is polynomial in n, log p, log(1/eta), and the full rational/basis encodings. A single random draw costs m bits; n primitive-root candidates are explicitly bounded.

The source list-recovery recursion still scans F_p for roots of Q_node(0,Y). Primitive-root acquisition does not eliminate that separate numeric-p loop. This claim expressly retains that cost for LIST. No polynomial-bit finite-field factorization theorem is imported. Likewise this implementation does not construct primes or certify a promised p on composite inputs. The distinction prevents a change of code cost from being mistaken for a stronger inherited-radius classification.
