## Objects and joint laws

Let n=2^m with m>=3. Choose 1<=j<=m-1, f=2^j, N=n/f, s=m+1-j, and an odd integer prime q such that v2(q-1)=s. In particular s>=2. Let

R=F_q[X]/(X^n+1),  R'=F_q[T]/(T^N+1).

Let A be the set of all N roots of T^N+1 in F_q. For K subset A of size k, 1<=k<=N, let I'_K be the ideal of R' supported on K in its evaluation CRT. Let J_K be the ideal of R generated by product_(a in K)(X^f-a), and set I_K=Ann_R(J_K). Thus R/J_K has intended degree D=kf. All quotient and ideal conventions are part of the statement and must be checked.

Fix b,tau,sigma>0 with integer b>=1, sigma>=2 tau n b. Define D_t(z)=exp(-pi z^2/t^2)/sum_(u in Z)exp(-pi u^2/t^2), and use coefficientwise independent draws, reduced modulo q, wherever a Gaussian in a finite ring occurs. All Gaussian coefficients, source points, target secret bits, and algorithmic tapes are independent except for the shared hidden K and deterministic maps explicitly specified below.

The **source** is one 2f-component challenge in R': draw K uniformly among all k-subsets of A, independently uniform W_0,...,W_(2f-1) in I'_K conditional K, and independently coefficient D_tau noises Z_0,...,Z_(2f-1). Publish the tuple (W_r+Z_r)_(r<2f). Its comparator is 2f independent uniform R' elements. Neither world contains a binary secret. The computational source premise is decisional pseudorandomness of this exact tuple distribution. No hardness for a two-component R' challenge is assumed to imply this premise.

The **target** is one pair (a,aS+E) in R^2, where a is uniform R, S has n independent uniform {0,1} coefficients, and E has n independent D_sigma coefficients, independent of a,S. Its comparator is uniform R^2. Advantages are absolute acceptance-probability differences. Public ring arithmetic has its usual polynomial bit cost in n,log q. Each source test below receives exactly one 2f-component challenge and evaluates its target distinguisher once.

Define Pack(B_0,...,B_(f-1))=sum_(r<f) X^r B_r(X^f) in R. Pack is an F_q-linear coefficient permutation, with no assertion that it is a product-ring homomorphism.

For a target test D, define public source tests:

1. T1: pack the first and last f input elements as (a,c); draw independent coefficient-binary s and coefficient D_sigma e; output D(a,as+e).
2. T2: pack as (a,c); draw independent coefficient-binary s and coefficient D_rho noise w, rho=sqrt(sigma^2-tau^2); output D(a,as+c+w).

## Explicit analytic premises for the finite implication

Let delta_F be the probability that an n-vector of independent integer D_tau coordinates leaves [-b,b]^n. Let F be that vector conditioned on the box, reduced in R. Let U,V be independent uniform I_K conditional K. Let G have independent coefficient D_tau law. Let H_F be the fixed proof-only canonical sequential Gaussian completion for B_F=sigma^2 I_n-tau^2 M_F M_F^t, sampled independently of S,G conditional (K,U,F). Here M_F is the integer negacyclic multiplication matrix. Assume the complete retained law satisfies

TV((K,U,F,FG+H_F),(K,U,F,E))<=eta_N,

where E is independent coefficient D_sigma noise. No assertion about covariance alone suffices.

Let Y'=S'+G' in R', for independent coefficient-binary S' and coefficient D_tau G'. Suppose that for every k-subset K of A,

TV((Y',ev_K(S')),(Y',Z'_K))<=epsilon_X,

where Z'_K is uniform F_q^k and independent of Y'. This is a smaller-ring, conditional, SAME-observation extraction premise, not an unconditional entropy bound.

Assume TV(D_tau^n+D_rho^n,D_sigma^n)<=eta_br for independent coefficient draws and wrapping. These finite premises may be instantiated by imported NC1 v2 and X1 v1 after all their guards and hypotheses are checked.

## Claims to check

B1-ALG-v1: ord_(2n)(q)=f, and X^n+1 has exactly N irreducible factors X^f-a, a in A. The quotient R/J_K is a product of k fields of order Q=q^f, with total F_q degree D=kf.

B1-PACK-v1: Pack maps f independent uniform I'_K elements exactly to a uniform I_K element. Grouping the source tuple into two packs gives exactly (U+F_infty,V+H_infty), with independent coefficient D_tau noises. Its uniform comparator maps exactly to uniform R^2.

B1-ISO-v1: For U uniform I_K and Z uniform R/J_K independent conditional K,U,

TV((K,U,UZ),(K,U,V))=1-(1-Q^-1+Q^-2)^k<=k/Q.

This is a retained-variable equality. No public equality after deleting K,U is claimed.

B1-X-v1: Under the smaller-ring extraction premise, for arbitrary distribution on K and arbitrary kernel C conditional K independent of S,G conditional K,

TV((K,C,Y,pi_K(S)),(K,C,Y,Z_K))<=min(1,f epsilon_X),

where Y=S+G in R and Z_K is independent uniform R/J_K conditional K. This statement uses coefficientwise independent S,G in R.

B1-DEC-v1: Under the analytic premises, the two explicit public tests satisfy

Adv_target(D)<=Adv_source(T1)+Adv_source(T2)
              +2eta_N+min(1,f epsilon_X)
              +1-(1-q^-f+q^-2f)^k+2delta_F+eta_br.

Approximate target-Gaussian sampling with error xi_E and broadening sampling with error xi_W add at most 2xi_E+xi_W; implemented source and target distribution errors add 2xi_source+xi_target. Binary and packing operations are exact. No completion kernel is executed by either test.

## Asymptotic proposal to check

For sufficiently large m, set j=ceil(log2 m), f=2^j, N=n/f, ell=log2 N, k=floor(sqrt(N)/ell^4), D=fk, b=ell^2, tau=ell, sigma=2n ell^3. Set A0=n^3+1+2N and M0=2n^3, and choose the least prime q congruent A0 modulo M0.

Use Xylouris, Acta Arithmetica 150 (2011), Theorem 1.1: the least prime in a coprime progression modulo M is at most C M^5.18 for an effective absolute C. It is claimed that q>n^3, q<=n^16 eventually, v2(q-1)=log2(2N), q<=N^17 eventually, and q/sigma>=n eventually.

Imported X1 v1: for every fixed K0>1, sufficiently large power-of-two N, each split prime q<=N^K0 and ceil((ln N)^2)<=k<=floor(sqrt(N)/(ln N)^4), tau=ceil(log2 N) gives epsilon_X<=(1/2)exp(-c_K0 N/(k(ln N)^6)). Appending source variables independent of the secret and auxiliary Gaussian retains this bound.

Imported guarded NC1 v2, applied to n-dimensional multiplication with sigma>=2tau n b, gives negligible eta_N at tau=ell; diagonal broadening gives negligible eta_br; Gaussian box truncation gives negligible delta_F. These are mathematical dependencies, with no source hardness consequence inferred from them.

It is claimed that all losses in B1-DEC-v1 are negligible in n, D log q=o(n), all inputs have polynomial bit length, prime search/root setup/packing/arithmetic and approximate samplers can be implemented in time polynomial in n, and f=Theta(log n) so the source uses 2f=Theta(log n) actual points in dimension N.
