# C03 — Rényi-stable transport from planted two-block binary ring knapsack

**Status: retired as an independent hardness direction; quantitative one-sample transfer retained.** The source-hardness bridge is unresolved. The transport calculations below survived bounded nonauthor criticism and coordinator reconciliation; the source-task attribution was corrected. No complete hardness theorem or formal proof certification is claimed.

## 1. Precise object and statement

Let n grow through powers of two, q>1 be odd and polynomially bounded in n, and R_q=(Z/qZ)[X]/(X^n+1) in its coefficient basis. Sample independently a_1,a_2 uniform R_q and x,y uniform {0,1}^n, and publish

\[
t=a_1x+a_2y\pmod q.
\]

The **planted** task is to recover this original pair (x,y). The **any-preimage** task allows any binary pair satisfying the equation. Let T recover the iid binary secret in exactly one reference Gaussian-error ring sample with success epsilon, where D_sigma(z) is proportional to exp(-pi z^2/sigma^2). Define rho_q=Pr[a_2 is a unit].

**Fully written small calculation — original map.** On the unit branch, put A=a_2^(-1)a_1 and C=a_2^(-1)t. Output (A,C+G_sigma mod q). Running T and recovering y=C-Ax yields planted source success at least

\[
\rho_q\epsilon^2
\left(\frac{1+\exp(2\pi/\sigma^2)}2\right)^{-n}.
\]

**Fully written small calculation — centered improvement.** With H=1+X+...+X^(n-1), instead output

\[
A'=2A,\qquad B'=2C-H+G_\sigma
    =A'x+(2y-H)+G_\sigma\pmod q.
\]

For tau=pi/sigma^2, source planted success is at least

\[
\boxed{\quad
\rho_q\epsilon^2
\bigl[1+e^\tau(\cosh(2\tau)-1)\bigr]^{-n}.
\quad}
\]

This preserves every nonnegligible epsilon with polynomial loss when n/sigma^4=O(log n), improving the original sufficient condition n/sigma^2=O(log n). The displayed likelihood factor is constant when sigma^4=Omega(n). These are thresholds for the stated certificates, not general noise barriers. The reference restriction q/sigma>=n^c must hold simultaneously. The same lower bounds imply any-preimage success, without equating the two tasks.

## 2. Mechanism and substantive difference

The source equation is normalized publicly by one invertible ring element; adding Gaussian noise gives a nearby binary-secret channel while preserving the actual source secret. The proof compares likelihood ratios for the entire secret-and-solver-input experiment, so successful target recovery transfers multiplicatively. It does not require negligible total variation, secret entropy extraction, or a lossy public sampler.

Centering is substantive: the residual coefficients become independent uniform signs. Their first-order perturbations cancel in the mixture, and the order-two likelihood cost becomes 1+O(sigma^-4) per coefficient. This improves the bound at growing widths; it is not asserted to improve the exact moment at every fixed small width.

## 3. Reference-target ledger

| Item | Exact accounting |
|---|---|
| Ring and degree | R_q=(Z/qZ)[X]/(X^n+1), same growing power-of-two n |
| Modulus | Same odd polynomial q; primality is unnecessary |
| Rank and basis | One rank-one target equation, coefficient basis unchanged; two hidden source blocks do not mean target module rank two |
| Secret | Actual planted x remains iid uniform binary |
| Error | Target is iid coefficient D_sigma, wrapped modulo q. Simulated error is iid y_j+G_j, or centered (2y_j-1)+G_j; equality with target error is not asserted |
| Gaussian normalization | exp(-pi z^2/sigma^2); sigma is width, not standard deviation. The added centered-map Gaussian still has width sigma |
| Sample count | Exactly one target ring sample per source instance; one call to T. No polynomial independent-sample conversion |
| Public scaling | Multiplication by 2 is a permutation because q is odd; A' is exactly uniform |
| Abort | Abort only on a_2 nonunit. Exact rho_q and polynomial lower bound below |
| Runtime and precision | Ring arithmetic and unit inversion are polynomial time. For effectively specified polynomial sigma, negligible Gaussian sampling error eta can be achieved in polynomial time and charged as rho_q max(0,epsilon^2/K-eta) |

For d_p=ord_(2n)(p), the unit probability is

\[
\rho_q=\prod_{p\mid q}(1-p^{-d_p})^{n/d_p}
\ge 2^{-\omega(q)}\ge q^{-\log_3 2}.
\]

This follows because every irreducible factor of X^n+1 modulo p has degree d_p and p^(d_p)>=2n+1. Each prime's unit factor exceeds 1/2. For prime q, rho_q>=1-n/q is also available; q>2n gives a simpler constant lower bound. Factorization of q is not needed by the algorithm: determinant and adjugate arithmetic in the public multiplication matrix suffice.

## 4. Source assumption and classification

K2 is the independently defined sampler in Section 1. Computational hardness of either planted recovery or any-preimage inversion for its exact parameters would be an assumption. Hardness of any-preimage inversion is the stronger and more conventional source claim sufficient for the reduction; exact planted recovery could also be hard because of ambiguity, so the success notions must remain separate.

**Classification: tailored relative to the checked independent source evidence.** Conditioned on a_2 being a unit, K2 normalizes exactly to (A,C=Ax+y), with an additional independent uniform unit a_2. It is therefore already a one-sample binary-secret, binary-error RLWE problem. The Gaussian comparison is useful, but renaming this neighbor “compact knapsack” does not independently support its hardness.

For any fixed public weights, planted targets have support size at most 4^n. A uniform target lies in that support with probability at most (4/q)^n. Thus for q>4 the planted and uniform-target joint distributions have statistical distance at least 1-(4/q)^n. This is a support calculation, not an efficient distinguisher or an attack.

## 5. Bridge and complete probability accounting

The arrow is **K2 planted recovery <= one-sample binary-secret Gaussian RLWE recovery**, with the stated success degradation. The simulator needs only (a_1,a_2,t), Gaussian coins, and T; it never needs x or y to create the query. Given T's x_hat, it computes y_hat=C-Ax_hat, checks binary membership and the original equation, and outputs the pair.

For P=D_sigma on Z, Q=(P shifted by +1 + P shifted by -1)/2, the exact identities are

\[
\frac{Q(z)}{P(z)}=e^{-\tau}\cosh(2\tau z),\qquad
M_2(Q\|P)=\cosh(2\tau).
\]

The second equality uses integer shifts of the Gaussian normalizing sum. Since Q>=e^(-tau)P,

\[
M_2(P\|Q)
=1+\chi^2(P\|Q)
\le 1+e^\tau\chi^2(Q\|P)
=1+e^\tau(\cosh(2\tau)-1).
\]

Independent coefficient products tensorize, and deterministic wrapping modulo q contracts M_2. Appending the shared independent (A',x) and solver tape, followed by the common transcript map, gives the same bound on the joint law relevant to F={T(A',B')=x}. Cauchy–Schwarz gives Pr_sim[F]>=Pr_target[F]^2/M_2(target||sim). The first identity alone would have the wrong divergence direction; the pointwise domination step supplies the needed reverse bound.

The actual source x is retained throughout. Marginalizing y is legitimate because the event of returning the planted pair equals x_hat=x pointwise; the stored C then recovers the original y. This is not a marginal-transcript-only argument or recovery of an independent posterior draw. Exposing C or y to T would change the target and is outside the improved mixture comparison.

There is no decision reduction and no source of m>1 independent target samples. Repeated Gaussian additions reuse A and y; ring rotations are correlated. An algorithm succeeding with polynomially many independent samples need not succeed with one, so the result does not cover that broader reference sample budget.

**First missing hardness step:** a justified reduction from an independently supported source regime to exact K2. No such bridge is supplied.

## 6. Best supporting evidence

Primary mathematical support is the complete calculation in [transport.md](C03/transport.md), including the original exact integer-shift/convexity bound, centered bound, wrapping proof, planted-event coupling, composite-q units, and finite success accounting.

The independently checked literature does **not** fill the first gap. Micciancio's inversion theorem uses cyclic convolution, growing coefficient width, and unbounded block count ([Theorems 4.2 and 4.8](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~daniele/papers/Cyclic.pdf)). Lyubashevsky–Micciancio supports negacyclic rings but proves collision hardness under coefficient-domain and compression hypotheses absent here ([Theorem 5.1](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~daniele/papers/IdealHash.pdf)). Exact source checks and access limitations are in [source-check.md](C03/source-check.md).

**Bounded experiment, secondary only:** the coordinator's [E03 record](../experiments/centered_noise_renyi.json) checks scalar wrapped moments at sigma=1,2,4,8,16,32. It agrees with the exact forward-moment identity within floating-point precision and illustrates the large-width improvement; centered noise is worse in its two smallest-width examples. It is a scalar truncation diagnostic, not an asymptotic proof or independent source-hardness evidence. This investigator ran no experiment.

## 7. Strongest objection and disposition

The exact source is a nearby RLWE assumption, and the checked compact-knapsack theorems do not justify its two-binary-block regime. In Micciancio's proof, the uniform-target regularity step conflicts with K2's exponentially sparse planted support. In the collision theorem, changing a wider domain and collision task to binary planted inversion has no reduction. These objections outweigh a correct transport formula.

**Disposition:** retire C03 as an independent explanation of the reference target's hardness. Retain the explicit multiplicative noise-transfer lemma, including the centered improvement and its one-sample scope. This does not assert that K2 is easy or that every future source bridge is impossible. Mathematical correctness of the transport and independent support for source hardness remain separate claims.

## 8. One small next question

**Unproved, not investigated:** in the centered map, choose the added Gaussian width s to compensate for the variance of the sign shift, with s^2 approximately sigma^2-2pi or exact discrete-variance matching. Does

\[
M_2\!\left(D_\sigma\middle\|
\tfrac12(D_s+1+D_s-1)\right)=1+O(\sigma^{-8})
\]

hold with an explicit uniform bound as sigma grows? The notation denotes the two translates of D_s. A proof would improve the quantitative transfer to n/sigma^8=O(log n); a surviving order-sigma^-4 term would refute that proposed gain. Wrapping would follow from the existing contraction argument.

This is a bounded next lemma about the source-to-target comparison. It would not independently change K2's unsupported hardness classification; that still requires the separate source-regime bridge. No small noise experiment can certify that bridge.
