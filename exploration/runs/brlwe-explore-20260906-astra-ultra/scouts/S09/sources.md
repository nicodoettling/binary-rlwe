## S1 — source-verified result

Feldman, Grigorescu, Reyzin, Vempala, Xiao, *Statistical Algorithms and a Lower Bound for Detecting Planted Cliques*, ECCC TR12-064 revision 2 (2016), later JACM 2017.

Primary PDF: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2012/064/revision/2/download/

Verified Definition 2.2: STAT(tau) returns an additive-tau estimate of the expectation of any query taking values in [-1,1]. Verified Definitions 2.5–2.6 and Theorem 3.2: the lower-bound method requires control of average correlations in every sufficiently large subfamily, with an explicit solution-set parameter for randomized search. It does not suffice to show that a random pair has small correlation. Section 3.2, Corollary 3.12 provides a pairwise-correlation route. No RLWE theorem is claimed by this source.

## S2 — source-verified result

Boudgoust–Lapiha, *Leftover Hash Lemma(s) Over Cyclotomic Rings*, ePrint 2025/1080, EUROCRYPT 2026.

Primary manuscript: https://hal.science/lirmm-05396888v2/document

Local verified text: R/sources/2025-1080.txt, lines 1023–1038, Lemma 11; and Section 3.1/Theorem 4. Lemma 11 says multiplication of a fixed vector by a uniform matrix over a finite ring is uniform over the module generated by the entries' ideal. It validates the ideal-image ingredient behind the scout's independent Fourier calculation. Its LHL theorems are not SQ or cryptographic lower bounds.

## S3 — source-verified result

Steiner, *The Complexity of Algebraic Algorithms for LWE*, arXiv:2402.07852v3.

Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2402.07852v3

Verified Definition 3.1 defines degree of regularity using the highest homogeneous parts. Theorem 4.2 applies to full-rank affine-derived systems, and Corollary 4.4 bounds solving degree by the Macaulay bound. Section 5.2 reduces binary-secret equations modulo x_j^2-x_j; equation (31) is a necessary dimension count, not a generic rank theorem. The abstract and introduction explicitly state that the LWE degree of regularity remains unknown; Section 5's favorable estimates assume an ideal scenario. These statements do not justify importing semi-regularity into ring matrices. The scout does not rely on the paper's numeric complexity estimates or Gaussian normalization.

## S4 — source-verified result

Albrecht, Cid, Faugere, Fitzpatrick, Perret, *Algebraic Algorithms for LWE*, ePrint 2014/1018.

Primary PDF: https://eprint.iacr.org/2014/1018.pdf

Verified Section 3 (equation (4)) uses a polynomial with all allowed errors as roots. Section 5, Assumption 1 explicitly assumes semi-regularity. Section 7 acknowledges the absence of a full proof and reports experiments. Neither the assumption nor its experimental support establishes the corresponding ring assertion. Their sigma is a standard deviation; no numerical bounds are transferred to the target's D_sigma width.

## S5 — source-verified result, version caution

Arora–Ge, *Learning Parities with Structured Noise*, ECCC TR10-066 (2010), predecessor of *New Algorithms for Learning in Presence of Errors* (ICALP 2011).

Primary early manuscript: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2010/066/download/

Verified Section 5 constructs polynomial constraints from bounded errors, linearizes them, and proves uniqueness in Theorem 5.3 using independent scalar samples. Theorem 5.6 states a subexponential small-error LWE consequence in its normalization. The early text's rounded Gaussian is different from the target's exact discrete D_sigma; we use the algebraic construction only, and derive our own wrapped-noise tail accounting.
