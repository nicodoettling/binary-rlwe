3. FF3-TWOSECRET-v1 — proof

Write d_s=s1-s0. The map (a,b) -> (u,v)=(b-a*s0,b-a*s1) is bijective,
because a=(u-v)/d_s and b=u+a*s0. Under label 0, u=e and v=e-a*d_s.
Conditional on each e, v is uniform, so the complete joint law is
nu_Q(u) times U_Q(v). Under label 1 it is U_Q(u) times nu_Q(v).
Under the source comparator it is U_Q times U_Q.

The two independent H calls therefore yield respectively
nu_q times U_q, U_q times nu_q, and U_q times U_q. Inverting with the
target unit t1-t0 gives exactly the displayed target law for the same
unknown label, including independence of the public multiplier and full
target noise vector. This proves the entire joint noise law, not moments.
Every row is normalized because it is a composition of bijections and
normalized independent kernels. Independent executions have the product
conditional law for every fixed label; integrating one shared label
preserves the shared-secret batch. No sample resamples the secret.

There are exactly two H calls plus ring operations. Their implementation
cost model is inherited rather than invented. Only two secrets are
supported; for binary n>=2 this does not supply the full 2^n-secret family.

4. FF3-ENUM-v1 — proof and resource accounting

For a nonzero binary difference d=s-t, multiplication by d over the
integer coefficient lattice has nonzero determinant. Indeed X^n+1 is
irreducible for power-of-two n (Eisenstein at 2 after X -> X+1), so a
nonzero polynomial of degree<n is nonzero in the field Q[X]/(X^n+1).
Every column of its negacyclic multiplication matrix has Euclidean norm
||d||<=sqrt(n). Hadamard gives 1<=|det M_d|<=n^(n/2). Smith normal form
then bounds the kernel modulo prime Q by
  |ker M_d mod Q| = product_i gcd(Q,a_i) <= product_i |a_i|
                  = |det M_d| <= n^(n/2).
This argument does not assume that d is a unit modulo Q.

Uniform A*d is uniform on the image, so any set of at most (4T+1)^n
residual values has probability at most
  n^(n/2) (4T+1)^n / Q^n.
When the true integer error lies in [-T,T]^n and another candidate passes,
A*(s-t) has a representative in [-2T,2T]^n. Since 4T+1<Q this box has
exactly (4T+1)^n residues. Union over the 2^n-1 alternatives yields etaQ.
No independence between competing candidate events was assumed.

For one Gaussian coordinate, normalizer>=1 and monotonicity/integration
give the safe estimate
  Pr(|E|>T) <= (2+sigmaQ^2/(pi*T)) exp(-pi*T^2/sigmaQ^2).
Union over coordinates gives tailQ. Hence the true s is the unique survivor
except with probability at most tailQ+etaQ. Couple the output to a fresh
target sample with the SAME s, using the same fresh output coins whenever
the correct unique survivor is found. This bounds the full output TV,
including public multiplier and all error coordinates.

On comparator input, for each fixed t the residual B-A*t is uniform, so
the chance any candidate passes is at most betaQ. Couple to a fresh target
comparator whenever none passes. This proves the comparator bound even
though the exceptional output can have an arbitrary distribution.

All these estimates are pointwise in fixed s. Condition on the single
shared S before taking k products; a coupling/union bound gives the stated
k-fold errors, capped by one. The error is not charged only per marginal
after discarding S. With finite scalar samplers of total per-output error
xi, another coupling adds k*xi to the full batch bound.

For T=ceil(sigmaQ*n), the tail has a polynomial prefactor times exp(-pi*n^2)
when sigmaQ has polynomial value. Under Q>=16n(4T+1), etaQ is at most
(1/(8 sqrt(n)))^n, and betaQ is also negligible. These estimates are uniform
enough for every fixed polynomial sample exponent, with one channel family.
Also Q>=64 sigmaQ n^2, proving sigmaQ/Q<=1/(64n^2). Increasing Q enforces a
separately fixed positive target noise reserve. A polynomial-value Q is an
additional supplied family when available; no prime generator or decoder
is hidden in this statement.

The algorithm really enumerates 2^n candidates, each with polynomial
arithmetic work. One retained candidate and a zero/one/many counter suffice
for polynomial memory. Exact target Gaussian sampling defines a kernel;
bounded finite sampling requires its explicitly charged approximation and
bit cost. Uniqueness of the survivor gives no efficient search algorithm.

5. FF3-COMPRESSED-SELECTOR-INFORMATION-v2 — proof

Use the randomized-class formulation; the deterministic one is its point
mass special case. Conditional on a fixed input x, one proposal/class has
law mu(e)R(c|x,e). The success probability is
  sum_e,c mu(e)R(c|x,e)G(e,c)/M = 1/M.
Every complete surviving retry history has this same next-trial law,
because e, the class draw, and acceptance coins are all fresh conditional
on x. Thus trials are geometric with mean M, and the complete ACCEPTED law is
  Q_x(e,c)=mu(e)R(c|x,e)G(e,c).
There is no omitted Bayes denominator: the sum is exactly one. Branches
with zero proposal or zero weight simply have zero accepted mass.

For every x,e,c, Q_x(e,c)<=M mu(e). Averaging over any X|S=s preserves this
pointwise domination. For any prior pi on N secret values,
  P_guess(S|E,C)
    = sum_e,c max_s pi_s Q_s(e,c)
    <= M * (#classes) * max_s pi_s
    <= M L max_s pi_s.
The output factorization is exactly the Markov condition needed for data
processing of guessing probability. It excludes input state, trial history,
advice, and correlated extra coins from the output channel. The trivial
upper bound one supplies the displayed minimum.

Let ell_C<=L be the actual class-alphabet size and use reference law
rho(e,c)=mu(e)/ell_C. On its support Q_s/rho<=M ell_C. Therefore
  D(Q_s || rho)<=log2(M ell_C),
and the usual mixture identity for relative entropy gives
  I(S;E,C)<=sum_s pi_s D(Q_s||rho)<=log2 M+log2 L.
Data processing proves the claimed I(S;Y) bound. No posterior independence
is assumed. The argument also handles arbitrary (including continuous)
input X because its only use is averaging the bounded channel rows.

An estimator's success event in the retained pair (S,Y) changes by at most
eta under joint TV eta. Apply the target estimator to Y, then combine its
success >=1-delta-eta with the uniform-prior upper bound ML/N. This proves
(T). The estimator can be exponential; its existence does not give the
channel an oracle for S.

The Gaussian corollary follows from the independently proved ENUM estimate
on the target modulus q. All bounds concern fixed-secret laws and retain S.
With sigma polynomial and q>=16n(4T+1), delta_n is negligible, hence
ML>=2^n(1-delta_n-eta_n). Polynomial M and L cannot meet it. Fixed target
parameters/families precede the polynomial sample/advantage exponents.

For q=2, X^n+1=(X+1)^n over F_2. Exactly half of ring elements A have A(1)=1
and are units. On that event A^{-1}B=S if E=0, giving guessing success at
least (1/2)(3/4)^n and ML>=(1/2)(3/2)^n for exact transfer. Conditional on a
unit A and uniform S, AS is uniform, so
  I(S;B|A)=n-H(E)=n(1-h2(1/4)).
Nonunit contributions are nonnegative; averaging gives the displayed
information lower bound. With retained-secret TV eta and the same uniform
S, finite-alphabet conditional entropy continuity costs O(eta*n+h2(eta)),
so negligible eta does not remove its linear-in-n information gap. This
supplementary observation is not needed for the Gaussian conclusion; the
exponentially small guessing event alone does not justify an approximate
q=2 corollary.

11. BOUNDED-AFFINE-TV-v1 — independent spectral proof

Let T=M_X be the real signed shift. Then T is orthogonal and T^n=-I.
Write C=E[Delta Delta^T], d=tr C=E||Delta||^2, and
  A=(T+T^T)/2, H=sign(A).
For power-of-two n>=4 the eigenvalues of A are cos((2j+1)pi/n), none zero.
Their minimum absolute value is a=sin(pi/n)>=2/n. Thus H is a symmetric
orthogonal matrix commuting with T and H*A=|A|>=a I. The antisymmetric
part of H*T has zero trace against symmetric C, so
  tr(H*T*C)=tr(|A|*C)>=a*d.
Use the real quadratic statistic f(w)=w^T H w.

For cube S, use Walsh sign chi(s)=(2s0-1)(2s1-1). Expanding
W_s=Z-sum_i s_i T^i Delta, averaging chi kills the constant, linear, and
all other quadratic terms. Independence of (Delta,Z) from S is exactly
what makes their expectations common. The surviving term is
  E_S chi(S) E f(W_S) = (1/2) tr(H*T*C) >= a*d/2 >= d/n.
The same coefficient of the common Y expectation is zero. If W_s,Y lie
in the radius-R ball, f has range [-R^2,R^2], so
  |E f(W_s)-E f(Y)| <= 2R^2 TV(Law(W_s),Law(Y)).
Since the retained S law is uniform on both sides, its joint TV is exactly
the average of the conditional TVs. Consequently it is >=d/(2n R^2),
which is stronger than the claimed d/(n^3 R^2).

For the weight-w slice, consider event B that exactly one of positions
0,2 is occupied and exactly one of positions 1,3 is occupied. Its probability
is
  p_w=4*binom(n-4,w-2)/binom(n,w),
exactly the stated falling-factorial expression. Conditional on B and the
remaining coordinates, put s0=b,s2=1-b,s1=c,s3=1-c; b,c are independent
uniform bits. Let D=I-T^2. The two varying multipliers are D and T*D.
The corresponding Walsh coefficient of E f(W_s) is
  (1/2) tr(|A| D C D^T).
The minimum singular value of D is 2 sin(pi/n)=2a. Therefore this coefficient
is >=2 a^3 d >=16d/n^3. Applying the same TV bound to the four configurations
and averaging over the outside coordinates gives retained joint TV
>=8 p_w d/(n^3 R^2), stronger than the asserted 4 p_w version. Contributions
from outside B are nonnegative and may be discarded. This argument handles
all 2<=w<=n-2, including n=4,w=2.

For tails use f_R(w)=f(w)1_{||w||<=R}. Its range is still [-R^2,R^2], and
|E f(W_s)-E f_R(W_s)|<=eta_s, likewise for Y. Repeating the cube calculation
gives
  TV >= [d/n - E eta_S - eta_Y]/(2R^2),
which implies the advertised weaker bound
  [d/n^3 - E eta_S - eta_Y]/R^2.
For the slice, the corresponding bound is at least
  [16 p_w d/n^3 - E eta_S - eta_Y]/(2R^2),
and again implies the advertised weaker 4 p_w bound. No distributional
Gaussian assumption, independence of Delta and Z, or covariance matching
was used.

For the modular statement, condition the FULL Gaussian vector Y_G on
||Y_G||<=R. This event has positive probability, since zero has positive
mass and R>0. Denote the resulting common bounded law by Y. The map modulo
q is injective on integer vectors in the radius-R ball when R<q/2, so the
bounded lower bound is unchanged for W_s mod q versus Y mod q. Coupling
Y_G to its conditioning costs tau before reduction modulo q and at most
tau after reduction. The triangle inequality gives precisely the claimed
minus-tau statement and its slice variant. No coordinatewise conditioning
is substituted for the norm-ball event.

12. SV-RS-DECODE-v2 — recognition, code, decoding, radius

Let A_J be the supplied integer column basis. The least e with e*A_J^{-1}
integral is the lcm of its reduced denominators, so it recovers the additive
quotient exponent. Under the promise this is p. Rational matrix inversion,
integer gcd/lcm, and basis membership tests have polynomial bit complexity
in full input lengths. In the numeric-p version, one may test p and scan
field elements to find roots; scanning costs polynomial in the NUMERIC p.
For a candidate primitive alpha, evaluate every basis element at each
alpha^(2j+1). The common zeros give the support. Checking pR subset J and
the support dimension/determinant verifies the promised product ideal.
Trying all primitive roots and cyclic starting positions recognizes a
consecutive interval whenever one exists; a different primitive root need
not preserve an interval, so this search is necessary in the recognition
claim. It is polynomial in n, numeric p, and full input lengths.

Because p belongs to J, z=p*lambda is integral. In R/pR, evaluation at the
n distinct roots identifies the algebra with F_p^n. The condition
lambda*J subset R is equivalent to z*j=0 mod p for every j in J. Thus
z's Fourier evaluations vanish off the t-point support of J and are
arbitrary on it. For support j=0,...,t-1, Fourier inversion gives
  z_k = alpha^{-k} f(alpha^{-2k}) mod p,
where deg f<t (the factor 1/n is absorbed into f). The n evaluation points
alpha^{-2k} are distinct and the multipliers alpha^{-k} are nonzero.
This is a generalized Reed–Solomon code of dimension t and minimum distance
n-t+1: a nonzero polynomial of degree<t has at most t-1 roots, and that
bound is attained by a product of t-1 evaluation factors. A cyclic shift
of support changes only the displayed nonzero coefficient multipliers.

Round each coefficient of p*T to an integer. A wrong rounded coordinate
requires |p*w_k|>=1/2, regardless of tie convention. Parseval with the
