## SV2: source-viability gate

**Question.** Does the permitted worst-case family contain a defensible hard source at the exact parameters supplied by the reduction? Do not equate successful source classification with a hardness foundation. The current run's SV diagnostic can be achieved while SV2 remains unresolved.

**Entry evidence.** Reconcile the current run's final exact acceptances and residual cases before allocating new research. The present baseline is the [accepted symmetry/subfield classification](runs/brlwe-fixed-family-20261021-astra-ultra/coordinator/acceptance-v3.json), together with the [residual-family analysis](runs/brlwe-fixed-family-20261021-astra-ultra/workers/SV/residual-family-frontier-v1.md). The latter is an investigation note, not an accepted hardness claim. Later results may change this starting point. In particular, do not promote provisional growing-support or all-subfield statements merely because they appear in the run's notes.

**Exact obligation.** Keep the ordinary-basis ideal input, N(J)≤2^(ρn), canonical-infinity error model, and the actual delivered BDD radius, including sample/advantage/auxiliary-prime losses. For the accepted predecessor this is r=ε_*ℓ²/(1024 n K χ Qmax), with its original definitions and guards. A new radius, source distribution, supplied short vector, or sampling oracle changes the problem and requires a new comparison. Easy wide DGS is not automatically an algorithm for this BDD task.

The initial concrete target is the residual large-characteristic split-prime family J=(p,X−a), with aⁿ=−1 modulo p, p≡1 modulo 2n, and superpolynomial p≤2^(ρn). Here p is a source characteristic, distinct from the target RLWE modulus. Polynomial bit length is not polynomial numeric value. Check admissible asymptotic parameters, effective input construction where required, the exact inverse-ideal decoding model, and all existing decoding criteria before calling this a surviving candidate. Other residual composite families remain reserves; solving the prime family alone cannot establish that the entire worst-case family is easy.

**First discriminating work.** Attempt an effective short-member acquisition or direct decoder for that precise task, alongside an explicit reduction from an independently supported hardness problem. Any hardness map must preserve input access, radius, success and polynomial represented cost. Finding no decoder, superficial similarity to another problem, or restating the source assumption is not a positive hardness result. Because short members already exist by the binary-collision argument, distinguish their existence from efficient acquisition.

**Required assessment.** Produce an exact residual-family/algorithm table, the strongest justified hardness connection or its first missing implication, and a recommendation to continue, redesign or hold. New mathematical claims require frozen versions, nonauthor full-proof review and fresh statement-only review, with changed dependencies rechecked. Same-model review remains correlated scrutiny. A programme assessment must separate theorem status from judgments about plausibility.

| Outcome | Programme action |
| --- | --- |
| Reviewed, quantitatively compatible hardness connection | Recommend renewed investment in extensions, including ENT1, through an explicit subsequent allocation decision. Check that any entropy restriction retains the supported hard family. |
| A candidate family is efficiently decodable | Remove only that family from the hardness candidates. Examine a distinct residual family if there is a concrete new test; do not infer global easiness or binary-RLWE easiness. |
| Entire permitted source family is efficiently decodable at the delivered parameters | Retire this source regime as a hardness foundation. Preserve the reduction and algorithmic results; prioritize a new source regime or stronger reduction parameters before hardness-motivated extensions. |
| Concrete residual family and useful partial analysis, but no supported hardness connection | Report viability as unresolved. Further substantial allocation needs a named new ingredient and an explicit rationale; an ENT1 run may be chosen for independent mathematical value, but not represented as consolidating established hardness. |
| Inconclusive or interrupted assessment, or merely no attack found | Keep ENT1 on hold. Preserve the first unresolved obligation; no automatic repeat, extension or successor. A completed report does not pass the hardness gate. |
| Current run already settles the relevant obligation | Reconcile its exact evidence and route by the applicable outcome above; avoid duplicating the investigation. |
