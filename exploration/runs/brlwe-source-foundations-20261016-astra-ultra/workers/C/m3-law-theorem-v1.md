# C-M3-v1: joint comparator law and ideal-preserving expansion obstruction

Frozen 2026-10-16 21:19 UTC. Status: PROVISIONAL. Author: Track C.

## Probability spaces and scope

Let q be prime, q=1 mod 2n, and R=F_q[X]/(X^n+1), with n>=2 a power of two. Fix a public CRT root alpha. Let A=(A1,A2) be exactly uniform in R^2. Let Z denote independent local random coins, with any law. A public algorithm returns m>=3 ring elements C_j=f_j(A,Z). Assume pointwise, for every positive-mass (a,z), that every f_j(a,z) belongs to the ideal generated by a1 and a2. This covers fresh, correlated, hidden, public and seed-adaptive ring-linear coefficients. It excludes an additive term outside this ideal. Let U_m denote the exact product uniform law on R^m.

Claim C-M3-O1: TV(Law(C),U_m)>=q^-2-q^-m. The polynomial-time event E_alpha={c_j(alpha)=0 for every j} witnesses an acceptance gap at least this large. Thus for any fixed exponent K and q<=n^K this class cannot produce negligible-TV independent uniform multipliers; the lower bound is at least (1-1/q)n^(-2K) when m>=3. This is a scoped public-event obstruction, not an entropy argument.

For the more restrictive map retaining the original seeds, C1=A1, C2=A2, and C3=A1 R1+A2 R2 with arbitrary seed-dependent hidden R1,R2, the exact E_alpha probability is q^-2. Therefore the exact gap of this event is q^-2-q^-3.

## Full three-multiplier and target-error law

For fresh independent pairs (R_j1,R_j2) drawn from a distribution nu on R^2, set C_j=A1 R_j1+A2 R_j2 for j=1,2,3. Define

    K_a(c)=sum_(r1,r2) nu(r1,r2) 1[c=a1 r1+a2 r2].

Let S uniform in {0,1}^n and E1,E2,E3 have independent iid coefficient D_sigma laws, independent jointly of A and all R. Write mu_sigma for the wrapped coefficient Gaussian law on R. The full witness/multiplier/error law is exactly

    Pr[S=s,C=c,E=e]
      = 2^-n product_(j=1)^3 mu_sigma(e_j)
        * q^(-2n) sum_(a in R^2) product_(j=1)^3 K_a(c_j).

The target law replaces the final mixture factor by q^(-3n). The errors and S are exactly product-independent, while the multipliers form a mixture of conditional product laws and are generally dependent. Adding responses b_j=c_j S+E_j is a deterministic map. Since the transcript contains C, the event E_alpha and its gap survive in the actual public target transcript. Correct error marginals do not repair the multiplier dependence.

If the original seeds are retained and only the third multiplier is mixed, the joint law is instead

    Pr[S=s,C=c,E=e]
      =2^-n product_j mu_sigma(e_j) q^(-2n) K_(c1,c2)(c3).

If all mixing coefficients are also revealed, conditional on them each noiseless linear map has F_q rank at most 2n. Its image occupies at most q^(2n) of q^(3n) points, and conditional TV from product uniform is at least 1-q^-n. This conditional statement is not automatically the unconditional bound when coefficients are hidden; the public CRT event above handles hidden coefficients directly.

## Proof of the scoped obstruction

The CRT evaluation homomorphism ev_alpha maps an ideal generated by a1,a2 to the ideal generated by the two scalar evaluations in F_q. On the event a1(alpha)=a2(alpha)=0 this image is {0}, so every c_j(alpha)=0. The seed event has probability q^-2 because the comparator seeds are independent uniform elements. Consequently Pr[E_alpha]>=q^-2. Under U_m the m evaluations are independent uniform F_q values, so Pr[E_alpha]=q^-m. The definition of TV gives the claimed bound. If C1=A1 and C2=A2 then E_alpha is exactly the seed event, proving the equality assertion.

No hidden witness, active-coordinate conditioning, sequential product bound or change of measure enters. Z may depend on A through the map; its distribution can equally be described by an arbitrary public kernel. The event is computable from the published multipliers using the public alpha. Its cost is O(mn) field operations by Horner evaluation; standard field arithmetic is polynomial in log q.

## Regression scope and open review

UNIFORM-APPROX-COLLAPSE is respected: the proof directly supplies an inverse-polynomial observable gap for this narrowly specified class and makes no general inference from exact uniform preservation to approximate preservation. A fresh additive W can violate the ideal-membership premise and is not ruled out. Nor does the claim assert impossibility of computational indistinguishability at superpolynomial q, where q^-2 may be negligible.
