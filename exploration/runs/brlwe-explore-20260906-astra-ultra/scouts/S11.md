# S11 — Quantum Fourier sampling and its missing coherent resource

**Finding [refuted, restricted route].** The proposal “prepare an explicit polynomial sample-list state, Fourier-measure it, then decode classically” cannot yield a quantum-only polynomial-time attack. Its entire output law has a classical sampler with polynomial overhead.

**Ledger.** Retain module rank one; growing power-of-two degree n; odd polynomially bounded q; coefficient basis; independent uniform Boolean secret coefficients; and at most polynomially many independent ring samples. Errors have independent coefficients D_sigma(z) proportional to exp(-pi z²/sigma²), sigma=poly(n), q/sigma ≥ n^c, with wrapped masses W_sigma,q(r)=sum_k D_sigma(r+kq). Search and decision remain separate. Rotations remain correlated deterministic transformations.

**Mechanism and dictionary [source-verified result; deduction].** Abelian stabilizer algorithms extract dual characters using coherent actions: Kitaev specifies this interface in §1 and constructs Fourier eigenvectors in §4, equation (21), pp.18–19 ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/9511026)). Put N=q^n and let M_s be negacyclic multiplication. The graph G_s={(a,M_s a)} has annihilator H_s={(u,v):u=-M_sᵀv}. Since M_sᵀ=M_(s†), where s†(X)=s(X⁻¹), the equations preserve rank one. A full noiseless graph state would reveal these equations.

Classical samples permit ψ=sum_i α_i|a_i,b_i> on K distinct supplied pairs with explicit normalized amplitudes. Uniform amplitudes require only recursive rotations from public prefix counts, polynomial work, and no QRAM or sampling oracle. This adapts Grover–Rudolph's conditional-mass construction, pp.1–2, equations (2)–(6) ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0208112)); the adaptation is our deduction. Discarding a retained sample index would instead produce a mixture.

**Falsifiable question [fully written small calculation].** Does this state's additive Fourier measurement evade efficient classical sampling? With χ(t)=exp(2πit/q),

p(u,v)=N⁻²|sum_i α_i χ(u·a_i+v·b_i)|² ≤ K/N².

Propose uniform (u,v), compute the public phase sum F, and accept with probability |F|²/K. Character orthogonality gives mean acceptance 1/K and exactly the desired accepted law. Expected cost is K proposals, each evaluating K phases. Polynomial-precision arithmetic and truncating the geometric waiting time give any requested accuracy with polynomial overhead. This covers all outcomes and efficient classical adaptive processing of explicitly listed states.

A second diagnostic of this same mechanism is

Pr[H_s]=N⁻¹ sum_r |sum_(i:e_i=r) α_i|² ≤ K/N.

For uniform amplitudes and distinct A_i, its expectation is [1+(m-1)ρ^n]/N, where ρ=sum_r W_sigma,q(r)². Distinctness fails with probability at most m(m-1)/(2N). In the explicitly noiseless diagnostic departure E=0, annihilator mass is exactly m/N; full graph support gives 1. This is mass, not recovery probability: v=0 is uninformative. No experiment was run.

**Objection and missing implication [unresolved].** Polynomial circuits can generate exponential support. With one unit A, public C=BA⁻¹ permits the full graph state sum_x|x,xC>/√N; Fourier sampling then reveals C=S+EA⁻¹, already classically known. Coherent combinations share and transform errors. The negative result excludes neither general preprocessing nor joint measurements. An extension needs sample-derived coherent denoising with a proved joint wrapped-noise law. Hardness additionally needs an average-case reduction; binary-secret specificity does not follow here.
