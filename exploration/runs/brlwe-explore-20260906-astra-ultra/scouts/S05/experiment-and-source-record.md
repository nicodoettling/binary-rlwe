## Source-checked decoding objection added before completion

Primary source: Venkatesan Guruswami and Madhu Sudan, *Improved Decoding of Reed–Solomon and Algebraic-Geometry Codes* (1999), author-hosted manuscript https://people.csail.mit.edu/madhu/papers/1998/venkat-journ.pdf . Built-in web search accessed the author-hosted primary text at 09:33 UTC; no access failure. Source-verified result: degree-at-most-k polynomials can be list decoded in polynomial time from agreement greater than sqrt(kn). This is an objection to one instantiation of the existing ideal-perturbation family, not an extra candidate.

Own fully written translation: suppose q=1 mod 2n, zeta has order 2n, omega=zeta^2, and CRT support is J={a,a+1,...,a+rho-1} modulo n. Inverse evaluation gives
U_j=n^(-1) zeta^(-j) omega^(-aj) p(omega^(-j)), deg p<rho.
Thus dividing each public coefficient A_j by the known nonzero multiplier n^(-1)zeta^(-j)omega^(-aj) produces a Reed–Solomon received word. If q>2H, box noise F_j leaves a coordinate unchanged with probability exactly 1/(2H+1). Fix a constant eta>0. For rho/n < (1/(2H+1)-eta)^2 and constant H, independent concentration gives enough correct coordinates for Guruswami–Sudan, with overwhelming probability. Enumerate the polynomial-size output list and check its agreements. For a uniform public A and a fixed agreement threshold tn, a union bound over q^rho polynomials and at most 2^n agreement sets is q^rho 2^n q^(-tn), negligible when rho=o(n), t is constant, and q grows polynomially. Unknown interval start a has only n choices and can be enumerated. CRT support on arithmetic progressions with invertible step has the same issue. This does not establish decoding for arbitrary CRT subsets.

Clarification: 2^n/q for a rank-one ideal is the mean of fiber cardinalities over all q possible field outputs, including unoccupied ones. It is not automatically the occupied-output mean or the size-biased mean seen by a random binary secret. The entropy bound H(S|AS)>=n-log_2 q remains valid independently of surjectivity onto these outputs.
