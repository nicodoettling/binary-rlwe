# Computational gadget escape: scope refinement

This supplements micciancio2018-ring-interface-v1.md without changing its bytes. Author analysis; no theorem imported beyond its exact scope.

The support bound for (A,A M_S) refutes statistical uniformity of the displayed retained hash endpoint. Small support alone does not refute computational pseudorandomness. A replacement proof using a computationally pseudorandom endpoint must name an independent established source assumption or prove an effective reduction to one; an assertion that the new endpoint is pseudorandom is not a repair by itself.

For the particular retained noiseless FULL-RING hash there is a stronger, efficient witness. Let a be uniform in R_q, S uniform coefficient-binary, and c=aS. On input (a,c), test whether a is a unit; if so compute a^-1 c by polynomial extended Euclidean arithmetic and accept iff all its coefficients lie in {0,1}. Compare to (a,u), u independent uniform R_q. The planted acceptance is p_unit, and the comparator acceptance is p_unit(2/q)^n. Thus the gap is p_unit[1-(2/q)^n]. For split prime q, p_unit=(1-1/q)^n>=1-n/q. This endpoint is efficiently distinguishable for q much larger than n. The analogous sign-cube test works over odd q.

This argument requires the noiseless full-ring hash and a to be retained. It does not apply automatically when the hash/public matrix is hidden inside a noisy transposed label distribution. That hidden/noisy distribution is a distinct possible computational escape. Its exact full law, relation to standard source samples, and computational assumption would need to be supplied. Nor does the unit test justify conditioning away nonunit secrets in the binary target: their probability can be inverse polynomial at fixed polynomial q, precisely where very small advantages matter.

An S-dependent Gaussian gadget is also a distinct escape from FF1-COV-v1, whose perturbation law is required to be independent of S. The outer wrapper may sample S and use it to choose a gadget, provided the target conditional public/error law remains exact. Source-side elliptical noise depending on S could conceivably be obtained by adding fresh covariance to a narrower standard Gaussian. What is missing is the complete second endpoint and effective hardness reduction, including all S-dependence and normalizers. The entropy and unit witnesses reject specific statistical/noiseless endpoint substitutions, not this entire class of hidden noisy constructions.
