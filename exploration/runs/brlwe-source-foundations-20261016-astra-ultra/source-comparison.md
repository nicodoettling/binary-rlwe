For each fixedh, EX usesK=h+10, q=P(1,2n^K), and separately d=floor(sqrt(n)/ell^4) orfloor(n/(24Kell)). This changes the source assumption even when the first d formula is unchanged. It gives no hardness transfer from the September modulus. The target noise is substantially enlarged; see [parameter table](parameter-table.md).

Pure ideal-preserving linear mixtures fail the first m=3 comparator test: at any fixed root, both seed coordinates vanish with probabilityq^-2, forcing all outputs to vanish, whereas three independent uniform outputs have probabilityq^-3. Hiding mixture coins does not remove that event. EX's additive W is part of the actual public algorithm and is paid for in the planted noise bound. The proof establishes a full retained-seed law, not merely Gaussian marginals or covariance matching.

Generic normal-form ring regularity and the identity-column remedy already appear in [LPR2013](https://sites.cc.gatech.edu/fac/cpeikert/pubs/toolkit.pdf), Theorem7.4/Corollary7.5. [Brakerski–Döttling](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185.pdf) also discusses Gaussian rerandomization; its stated sample/source/secret conditions differ. [RS1](criticism/RS1/report-v1.md) records the precise targeted comparison and access qualifications. The proposed novelty is relative to this project's checked baseline, not a claim of exhaustive literature priority.
