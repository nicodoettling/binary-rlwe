FIXED-MAIN-v1 requires sigma0>=8*k0*n*(n/2+2)/epsilon_*. Thus its displayed asymptotic family fixes sample and inverse-advantage exponents b,c before choosing sigma0 and the target modulus range. It is not a universal assertion that one fixed noise/modulus family handles every polynomial sample count and every inverse-polynomial advantage. For supplied parameters, the exact finite guard determines the covered window. This limitation must remain visible in final scope; polynomial radius/cost alone does not erase it.

A tempting stronger repair would borrow scalar-LWE Gaussian randomized rounding with a constant-norm signed secret. For ring samples, constant coefficient norm does not ensure a spherical joint rounding error. Even T=(1,1,1,1) in Z[X]/(X^4+1) has negacyclic multiplication rows (1,-1,-1,-1) and (1,1,-1,-1), whose dot product is2. If independent spherical continuous rounding noise V has covariance cI, the first two coefficients of T*V have covariance2c, while each variance is4c. A scalar variance calculation cannot certify independent coefficient Gaussian errors. This is an explicit scope warning for that proposed shortcut, not an impossibility theorem for all randomized-rounding repairs. No such extension is a dependency of the present theorem.
