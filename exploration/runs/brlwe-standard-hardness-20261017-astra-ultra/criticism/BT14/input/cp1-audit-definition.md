# Administrative definition: CP-1 audit condition

CP-1 means that any sequential probability-product, restored-independence, or adaptive concentration claim must be justified with the actual causal filtration. A stage-t fresh-randomness or success bound must hold conditional on the complete preceding transcript on each relevant positive-probability branch; unconditional marginals do not suffice. Conditioning on future successes is not conditioning on the past and may change the claimed fresh law. Explicitly dispose of zero-mass branches, selected/aborted stages and every conditional normalizer. If a theorem uses only a causal conditional union bound, identify that argument and do not invent a product requirement. This is an audit criterion, not a supplied scientific theorem, proof or previous counterexample.
