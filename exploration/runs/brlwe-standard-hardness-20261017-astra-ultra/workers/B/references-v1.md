1. Lyubashevsky–Peikert–Regev, *A Toolkit for Ring-LWE Cryptography*, full version dated 16 May 2013. [Author-hosted PDF](https://sites.cc.gatech.edu/fac/cpeikert/pubs/toolkit.pdf), 51 pages, opened successfully through browser and read at printed pp.20–22 (Definitions 2.20–2.21, Theorem 2.22, Lemmas 2.23–2.24), and pp.6,28–30 for dual scaling/decoding basis. The exact hypotheses and equations needed here are paraphrased in first-test-v1.md §5. Applicable model is a quantum worst-case reduction; the spherical width depends on the supplied sample budget. For power-of-two cyclotomics, scaling dual b by n gives primal coefficient Gaussian width sqrt(n) times canonical width. Valid discretization preserves the comparator; nearest rounding is distinguished from exact D_tau. Direct ePrint access returned HTTP403 and Michigan author mirror timed out; these are access errors, not policy interruptions. No PDF bytes were downloaded or hashed.
