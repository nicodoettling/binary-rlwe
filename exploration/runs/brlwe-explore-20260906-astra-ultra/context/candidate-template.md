# Candidate dossier template

Status: provisional / retained with unresolved bridge / retired. Evidence is attached to individual claims; agreement is not confirmation.

1. Precise mathematical object or proposed statement, with all quantifiers and parameter restrictions.
2. Mechanism in ordinary mathematical language; identify the first substantive difference from the old framework.
3. Reference-target ledger: ring, degree, rank, modulus, basis, secret law, error law and normalization, independent ring sample count. Record every departure.
4. Source assumption, if proposed: independently define its sampler and search/decision task. Classify as established and independently studied, new but independently motivated and testable, or tailored. Do not rename the target problem and call that a source.
5. Bridge: reduction direction, public simulator, hidden information it needs, marginal and joint distributions, recovery map, statistical/computational distance, runtime/sample/noise/degree/rank/modulus losses. Identify the first missing step. If this is a structural diagnostic only, say so explicitly.
6. Best supporting evidence: source theorem/section and URL; fully written calculation; bounded experiment; heuristic; conjecture; unresolved. Separate our deductions from source claims.
7. Strongest objection and its disposition. An incorrect parameter regime, simulator, or reduction arrow outweighs favorable tiny examples.
8. One small next question, lemma, or experiment whose result would change the assessment. No extended theorem-proving effort in this session.

Do not infer hardness from uniqueness, posterior clusters, low-degree bounds, or an algorithm failing. A direction may survive as a diagnostic with no reduction; its unresolved bridge must remain prominent.
