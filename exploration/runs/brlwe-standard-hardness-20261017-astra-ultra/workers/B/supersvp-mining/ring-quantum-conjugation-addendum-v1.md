# Conjugation law clarification for ring-quantum-proof-v1

Status: PROVISIONAL notation clarification prompted by D's review; original bytes preserved.

Section2's phrase that a signed coefficient permutation preserves the law of coefficient-iid variables requires the one-coordinate distribution to be symmetric. In this application it is: f1 is real and even on centered Zq, hence |hat f1(t)|^2=|hat f1(-t)|^2. Therefore the exact Fourier product law is invariant under coefficient conjugation.

Alternatively, the moment argument may be applied to the uniform conjugated key a* directly, without claiming invariance of any arbitrary input law. The projection-pivot proof also works for independent, nonidentically distributed coefficients with a common maximum-atom bound mu. Norm preservation and the exact identity M_a^T=M_(a*) hold without symmetry. No numerical bound, endpoint law, or theorem hypothesis is changed by this clarification.
