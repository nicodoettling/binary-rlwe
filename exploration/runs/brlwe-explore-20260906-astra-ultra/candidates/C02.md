# C02 — CONTROL: actual transcript guessing and occupied Boolean cosets

Final disposition after bounded nonauthor criticism: **retained with unresolved bridge, as a structural diagnostic**. No binary-RLWE hardness theorem or public-transcript attack is established.

The useful repair is to analyze the actual wrapped transcript rather than infer its entropy from an unnecessarily informative secret-dependent precursor. Revealing public-sampler coins does not itself change the posterior under the correct joint conditional-error hypothesis. Exact occupied-coset accounting can improve a lattice leakage charge, but neither improvement presently supplies a public tuple that is both pseudorandom and lossy for the reference binary distribution.

## 1. Precise object and conditional target

Let n≥2 grow through powers of two, q≥3 be odd and polynomially bounded, and m=m(n) be a fixed positive polynomially bounded number of ring samples. Set R_q=Z_q[X]/(X^n+1), S←{0,1}^n uniformly in the coefficient basis, and independently sample all error coefficients from

    D_σ(z) = exp(-πz²/σ²)/Σ_{k∈Z}exp(-πk²/σ²).

Here σ is a coefficient width, with q/σ≥n^c for a fixed c>0 in the reference comparison region. Write W_{σ,q} for its wrapped law. For a public tuple a define the exact guessing functional

    g_{σ,q}(a)
      = 2^{-n} Σ_{y∈R_q^m} max_{s∈{0,1}^n}
           ∏_{i,j} W_{σ,q}(y_{i,j}-(sM_{a_i})_j).

**Unresolved proposed combination.** Find or analyze an efficiently sampled tuple law X with both joint computational indistinguishability X≈U(R_q)^m and

    Pr_{a←X}[g_{σ,q}(a)≤2^{-h(n)}]≥δ(n),

where h=ω(log n), δ≥1/poly(n), or with an explicitly negligible smoothing allowance. The old source sampler below has an independently described pseudorandomness route; this actual-transcript inequality is missing for binary S and the fixed discrete errors.

## 2. Mechanism and the first substantive change

**Fully written calculation.** If A=F(T), and the secret prior and *entire joint* conditional final error law given (S,A,T) depend only on (S,A), Bayes' formula gives

    Law(S|A,Y,T)=Law(S|A,Y).

In particular, it suffices that (S,E_1,...,E_m) is jointly independent of T with the reference product error law. Pairwise independence or individually correct error marginals is insufficient. Gaussian decompositions must be checked conditional on sampler coins, including cross-sample covariance. Revealing T can help an efficient algorithm without adding statistical information. [Proof and a pairwise-independence counterexample](C02/calculations.md), §1.

**Fully written calculation.** By contrast, take an integer denominator matrix F, its modular inverse H, HF=I+qK, and Λ=Z^nF. The old proof's richer real-torus precursor has form U=(SH+GF^{-1}) mod q. On ||S+G||_∞<q/2, compute v=uF, w=center_q(v), and J=(v-w)/q; then S≡-qJ mod Λ. For continuous coefficient width t, no-wrap failure is at most 2n exp(-π(q/2-1)²/t²). The entire noisy expression is covered. This is coset leakage **of the precursor**, not an observation available from the public transcript. [Proof](C02/calculations.md), §2.

The transcript is a Markov postprocessing of this precursor conditional on sampler coins, so its guessing can be strictly smaller. The first proposed change is to bound g_{σ,q}(a) after that postprocessing, using its wrapped likelihood. Merely hiding sampler coins cannot achieve this change.

**Fully written calculation.** For any fixed full-rank integer lattice Λ, let N_occ count its cosets meeting the Boolean cube. Then

    p_guess(S|S mod Λ)=N_occ/2^n,
    Htilde_∞(S|S mod Λ)=n-log_2 N_occ.

Thus the appropriate exact-coset charge is log_2 N_occ, rather than the ambient index. The unwrapped integer augmentation (S+G,G mod Λ) admits this leakage charge. The corresponding support-count argument does not automatically hold after reduction modulo q when Λ+qZ^n=Z^n. [Proofs and the weighted collision identity](C02/calculations.md), §3.

## 4. Independently defined source assumptions and sampler

**Source-verified framework, independently written sampler.** The old Section 6 construction uses two established, independently studied assumption families, with hardness asserted only at chosen source parameters:

* DSPR: sample Gaussian g and independently Gaussian f conditioned invertible modulo q, with canonical width γ (coefficient width γ/sqrt(n)); distinguish h=g/f mod q from uniform.
* Hermite-form RLWE for a source law χ: a secret s_0←χ and errors e_j←χ accompany uniform public h_j; distinguish (h_j,h_js_0+e_j) from uniform pairs. This source secret/error law is not silently identified with binary S and reference D_σ.

Sample f,g as above and independent r_i,r'_i←χ. Put z_i=gr_i+fr'_i and A_i=f^{-1}z_i=h r_i+r'_i. DSPR replaces the shared h by uniform; an m-step Hermite-form hybrid then replaces A_i by independent uniforms. A public simulator given h needs only fresh r_i,r'_i, not hidden f,g. This has polynomial runtime and an m factor in the HNF distinguishing loss, preserving degree, rank and modulus. [Source definitions, complete sampler and hybrid](C02/sources-and-bridge.md), §§1–2; [BD2020](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185.pdf), Definitions 2.6/6.1 and Lemma 6.6.

No independently justified source assumption for a new Boolean-lossy denominator distribution is proposed. Calling its desired public ratio pseudorandom by assumption alone would be tailored.

## 5. Bridge, quantifiers, and missing step

The reduction direction is source decision hardness → pseudorandom public-A tuple → a search-hardness implication, **provided** the matching actual-transcript entropy property is supplied. Generating the target transcript from public A needs only internally sampled S and reference E; recovery uses the same coefficient secret, without a degree/rank/modulus conversion.

**Source-verified boundary.** BD Definition 4.1/Theorem 4.2 give mild search hardness from sometimes lossiness. This excludes a solver that succeeds for almost every public A on a nonnegligible fraction of fixed (S,E) choices. It does not by itself say every solver's average success is negligible. A stronger bound E_{a←X}g_{σ,q}(a)≤negl would directly imply standard search hardness at the same fixed m by testing a solver on internally known S,E; this stronger average bound is also unproved. [Detailed reduction and definitions](C02/sources-and-bridge.md), §3.

The old Gaussian masking analysis is continuous, with lattice-discrete auxiliaries and explicit smoothing losses. It does not automatically yield the reference fixed iid discrete-Gaussian law. The norm-controlled source noise increase includes sqrt(m); no theorem for arbitrary polynomial m follows from a one-sample control. The old amplification uses an unbounded sample oracle, and its finite-input rerandomization changes the error law through subset sums. Decision hardness remains separate. [Source/error/sample ledger](C02/sources-and-bridge.md), §4.

**First missing step:** prove the displayed wrapped-likelihood bound for an independently supported pseudorandom public tuple law under the exact target errors, on a nonnegligible fraction of public tuples. No such bound is established here.

## 6. Best evidence and qualified controls

**Deterministic injection, fully written.** N_occ((f))=2^n if either σ_min(M_f)>sqrt(n) or |Norm(f)|>n^{n/2}. The first excludes short lattice vectors; the second uses norm divisibility and Hadamard. On the specific event ||σ(f^{-1})||_2≤24sqrt(n)/γ, γ>24n implies injection and hence precursor recovery outside its no-wrap tail.

**Normalization restriction.** No probability is attached here to that BD-displayed event. S06 flagged its norm/width transfer from SS2013, and C02 does not turn this into a source-proof audit. Separately, the directly checked SS projection bound gives unconditioned injection probability >1/2 when the *coefficient* width κ satisfies κ≥16nη_{1/4}(Z^n)/sqrt(2π) and κ>16sqrt(2)n. Conditioning on f being a unit requires a separate invertibility probability. [Deterministic and normalized calculations](C02/calculations.md), §§4–5; [SS2013](https://eprint.iacr.org/2013/004.pdf), Lemma 2.7 and the small-ball portion of Lemma 4.1.

**Low exponent replacement refuted by calculation.** For a known integer κ∈(f), coprime to q, and a rational ratio norm ≤L with κL<q/2, the public test ||center_q(κA)||_∞≤κL accepts the structured sample but accepts uniform A with probability ((2floor(κL)+1)/q)^n. A polynomially bounded hidden exponent can be enumerated with a polynomial loss. This rejects the direct f=1+X replacement, whose exact Boolean coset entropy is n-1. [Proof](C02/calculations.md), §6.

**New bounded algebraic control.** For n≥4 a power of two, f=1-X-X² has quotient (Z/L_{n/2}Z)^2, where L_k is the Lucas number. Its exponent grows exponentially, yet its index bounds the remaining Boolean coset entropy below by

    (1-log_2 φ)n-2log_2(1+φ^{-n}) ≈ 0.306n.

Also ||M_f||≤sqrt(5), so the old singular-value charge can exceed n while the exact index charge is about 0.694n. Thus positive coset entropy does not force a polynomial scalar exponent. The fixed public f still gives a short-numerator test via fA, and rotations/automorphisms form an enumerable family. This is a control separation, not a sampler proposal. [Complete two-dimensional quotient calculation](C02/calculations.md), §7.

**Experiment, root-owned E04.** At n=4,q=17,σ=4, the fixed-A discrete toy retains approximately 2.71229 bits in the actual wrapped transcript versus 2.79×10^{-6} in its precursor. At n=16,q=257,σ=16, the actual value is about 14.60059 bits. The reported precursor zero at the latter point is numerical rounding/truncation: the exact full-support law has positive residual entropy. Gaussian tails and the model are recorded in [code](../experiments/wrapped_precursor.py) and [output](../experiments/wrapped_precursor.json). C02 ran no experiment. A symbolic n-O(1)-bit counterpart is in [calculations](C02/calculations.md), §8.

## 7. Strongest objection and disposition

No current sampler combines pseudorandom public A with reference actual-transcript lossiness. The old sampler's informative precursor blocks that particular entropy lower bound; it does not refute the true transcript inequality. The explicit low-index denominators show why exact geometry matters, but their direct public samplers are distinguishable. E04 proves the precursor gap can be large for a fixed public multiplier, without repairing pseudorandomness. These objections outweigh the favorable controls. Retain this direction to guide the proof target and reject invalid repairs, not as an explanation of binary-RLWE hardness.
