# B review: source, effectiveness and complexity disposition v1

Nonauthor review only. No final acceptance. The eleven exact reviewed B files are copied under `input-v1/` and hashed before review.

1. The core upper bound is proved directly from an elementary completed-square identity and Poisson summation for a positive Gaussian. I reconstructed both uses of Poisson summation and all constants from the displayed formulas. The centered-theta maximum is also the same elementary identity already used in the accepted A-FRAC proof. No source theorem is being imported under an altered Gaussian normalization.
2. The residue lower bound is an exact finite Bayes calculation. It does not assume uniformly weighted Gaussian cosets. The observation is the entire unwrapped integer S+G, not its residue and not an accessible efficiently sampled public variable. All zero outcomes remain in the law.
3. The short-basis assertion uses only a binary collision, field invertibility of its rotations, and the exact primitive-flag argument in A-FRAC section5. It is an existence claim. Running that flag construction without knowing f would require an additional algorithm; none is asserted.
4. Computing C_J by listing all2^n vertices is exponential in dimension. A pointwise proof of a cube bound is allowed as a mathematical promise, while the executable same-J algorithm uses the supplied positive delta. There is no claim that the promise can be checked in polynomial time or that arbitrary input bases provide their collision witness.
5. The norm289 family has an explicit five-term polynomial F and an exact public multiplication basis. Its source test has polynomial bit cost in n and logq. At the frozen width, the irrational-looking threshold is tested by squaring integer coefficients, so no unbounded computable-real comparison is hidden. Multiplication by F before centered lifting is essential and correct.
6. The author's exact finite test uses integers/rationals of about8300 bits, not floating equality tests. Its truncation error is bounded in normalized joint TV using rho_t(J)>=1. Tails, zero and all enumerated candidates are preserved. Finite tests are not standard-hardness experiments.
7. For the changed-premise composition, D may be exponentially large numerically, while logD=O(n logn) is controlled by the collision norm bound. HNF/SNF and scalar input encoding costs are charged in bit lengths; no polynomial numerical D claim follows. Likewise the final width remains proportional to the numerical q and chi/delta choices. Polynomial encodings alone do not imply a polynomial approximation factor.
8. Current C8 theorem-v8 and its accepted rational final threshold were used. The all-input bounded recovery code, constructive dispatcher cap, actual compiled-circuit cost, setup/serialization and coherent invocation requirements remain unchanged. A classical service that only answers recovery queries is not substituted for executable code.
9. Root's new M1 sampler is a separate provisional dependency of the concrete family's delivered-DGS simulation sentence. This review does not certify M1 through B. The explicit public basis and the source attack already make the family an easy-source control without that dependency.

No external browsing, installation or new primary-paper survey was needed for these local statements.
