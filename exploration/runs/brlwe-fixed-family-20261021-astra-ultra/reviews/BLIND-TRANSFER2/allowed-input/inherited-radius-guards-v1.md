# Inherited radius parameter assumptions

Let n>=2 be a power of two, k0>=1, 0<epsilon_*<=1. The intermediate advantage parameters are epsilon0=epsilon_*/2, epsilon=epsilon_*/4 and delta=epsilon_*/16. For the finite positive integer physical batch K, set ell=ceil(log2(2nK)) and sigma_floor=8n^2 ceil(sqrt(Kn))ell^4. The inherited modulus guards are Qmax>=Qmin>=n sigma_floor. The positive integer chi>=1 is part of the supplied parameters. The radius is r=delta ell^2/(64nKchi Qmax)=epsilon_*ell^2/(1024nKchi Qmax).

These are definitions and assumptions, extracted from the fixed and averaged interfaces. They do not assert an unrestricted fixed-family theorem. The decoder may also be considered at an arbitrary supplied rational r under its own explicit guards. Eligible source ideals obey N(J)<=2^(rho n), for the fixed stated rho<1.
