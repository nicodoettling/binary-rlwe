## GAUSS-AVG-v1

**Verdict: PASS, exact continuous-Gaussian retained-secret scope.** Let H=Cov(Delta,Z). Direct expansion gives V(s)=B+M_s C M_s^T-M_s H-H^T M_s^T. On each face varying coefficients 0 and 1, the alternating second difference is TC+CT^T; background-secret terms and both cross-covariance terms cancel. The faces partition the entire cube. The triangle inequality gives a corner at Frobenius distance at least ||TC+CT^T||_F/4 from any fixed V_* on every face, hence a fraction at least 1/4. No probability multiplication is hidden here.

In a complex unitary eigenbasis of the real orthogonal negacyclic shift, C remains Hermitian PSD. Its diagonal entries c_j are nonnegative and sum to tr(C). The diagonal of TC+CT^T is 2 Re(zeta_j)c_j. For power-of-two n>=4, n is divisible by 4 and every |Re(zeta_j)|>=sin(pi/n). Consequently ||TC+CT^T||_F>=2 sin(pi/n) tr(C)/sqrt(n). A real symmetric discrepancy has an eigenvalue of magnitude at least its Frobenius norm divided by sqrt(n). Combining the factors gives a direction with variance difference at least sin(pi/n)tr(C)/(2n)>=tr(C)/n^2. The last inequality follows from sin(x)>=2x/pi on [0,pi/2]. This also holds at n=4; no large-n approximation was used.

For each such secret, take frequency 1/sqrt(L) in the selected direction. Gaussian characteristic magnitudes are exp(-v/(2L)), including degenerate Gaussians and arbitrary means. Both variances belong to [0,L], so the magnitude difference is at least exp(-1/2)|v-v_*|/(2L). A bounded complex test has expectation difference at most 2TV. Projection contracts TV. Conditional TV is therefore at least exp(-1/2)tr(C)/(4n^2 L). The common retained uniform secret makes joint TV exactly the average of conditional TVs, yielding the stated 1/16 constant. Selecting directions using s is legitimate only for this retained-secret comparison; it constructs no sample-only efficient distinguisher.

The sufficient L bound follows from Var(X-Y)<=2Var(X)+2Var(Y), ||M_s||_op<=n, and trace bounds on positive covariances. It requires no independence between Delta and Z. If C=0 the lower bound is zero, as it should be. At n=2 the Fourier real parts vanish. The preserved control C=B=I, H=(I-T)/2 gives equal covariance I at every secret, with positive Schur complement I/2; it excludes the missing degree exactly.

The Gaussian premise, common perturbation law and retained S are load-bearing. FRONTIER1-NONGAUSS-TV gives covariance trace 1 with retained TV only 3/3200000 at n=4. FRONTIER1-SECRET-ADVICE sets Z=M_S Delta and makes every residual zero. The theorem excludes both. Hamming-weight advice also invalidates the across-cube common-law premise; GAUSS-AVG must not be silently applied conditional on a weight-dependent kernel. WEIGHT-COV supplies a different, exact equality conclusion within each nontrivial weight slice.
