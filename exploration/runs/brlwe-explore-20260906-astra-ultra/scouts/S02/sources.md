- **Source-verified result.** Boudgoust–Jeudy–Tairi–Wen, *Hardness of M-LWE with General Distributions and Applications to Leaky Variants*, https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1472 , supplied text sources/2025-1472.txt, Section 1.2 p.6 and Theorem 5.1 pp.19–20 (lines 1701–1810). The HNF proof changes the secret to selected source errors, reduces sample count by d, and records a permutation of the joint error distribution. - **Own deduction, baseline comparison only, not a third retained mechanism.** In rank one, a source with one designated binary-error sample B0=A0U+E0 and remaining Gaussian-error samples would give an exact binary target by HNF when A0 is a unit: A'i=-Ai A0^{-1}, B'i=Bi+ A'i B0=A'i E0+Ei, with recovery U=A0^{-1}(B0-E0). But this changes one source error distribution to coefficient-binary and increases the source sample count by one. Independently establishing hardness of that mixed-noise source is precisely the missing premise; merely proposing it does not advance the current source-to-target reduction. The memo keeps only the two pre-release mechanisms.
