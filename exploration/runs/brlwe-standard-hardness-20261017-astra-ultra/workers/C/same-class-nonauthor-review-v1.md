## Scope and regression audit

This is a deterministic asymptotic counting argument. Its only probability interpretation is the proportion under the mathematical uniform measure on a finite ordinary class group. It neither constructs nor assumes an efficient sampler for that measure. There are no adaptive transcripts, postselection normalizers, sequential-product claims, or Gaussian changes of measure, so cached-prefix/CP-1 is inapplicable rather than silently presumed. Finite enumeration cannot validate the analytic asymptotic; exact local discriminant/Euler-factor controls are corroboration only. No new experiment was run for this review.

Multiplying an ideal by a nonzero field element preserves its ordinary class, including fractional intermediate ideals. Therefore an algorithm required to map EVERY class to an integral representative of norm exp(cn) by such multiplication cannot exist for all sufficiently large n. This does not prohibit mapping a chosen subclass, changing class, changing field or dimension, accepting a larger norm, using different binary encodings, or obtaining only a nonuniform average distribution. It gives no effective threshold, no hardness of the low-index family, and no completion or impossibility theorem for the research goal.
