Hypothesis: the exact repaired symmetry and subfield algorithms recover the odd part from an arbitrary ordinary basis, compute correct congruence indices, handle merged contracted primes by maximum valuation, and produce valid bounded members and exact decoders whenever their stated finite guards hold. The cyclotomic residue-degree inequality survives every tested odd residue class; removing its additive allowance fails.

Code: `controls-v1.py`, frozen and hashed before execution. Inputs: the seven literal deterministic fixtures in that code, with n=2,8,16,32; one unit ideal, a ramified power, a prime above 3, its ramified product, equal and unequal merged split-prime contractions, and a product with trivial stabilizer. Supplied bases are deliberately changed by a unimodular shear of magnitude 2^256+1. Fixture factorization is used only to generate test data; the algorithm under test receives the resulting matrix. Every contraction is checked by two independent normal-form index formulas, Smith and Hermite. Collision loops are run only when their numeric count is at most 20000; other index checks still run and the skip is recorded. Order controls cover N=2,...,10, every odd integer 3<=p<2^N, and every 2<=G<=N. Negative controls reject missing order allowance, ambiguous a, and a weak rounding endpoint. CP-1 exact inequalities are retained without introducing any sequential probability assertion.

No random seed: exhaustive/deterministic. Precision: exact integers and rationals only; elapsed time is not mathematical evidence. Resources: wall timeout 120 seconds, CPU 115 seconds, RLIMIT_AS 512 MiB. Python3 and existing SymPy only, no installation. One foreground job; no heavy job. A second job requires a new root grant and will be used only if necessary. The finite search is not a proof of the general theorem and does not instantiate the full DD parameter package. Root slot grant is required before running.
