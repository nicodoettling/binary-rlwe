## B1's exact source experiment and public reduction

Fix power-of-two n, split prime q, support size d, and a public law on d-subsets T of the n CRT roots (uniform in the main candidates). Put J={r:r vanishes at every root in T} and I=Ann(J), the ideal supported on T. Draw U with the specified base law: either uniform I when claiming the nearest original HLBDD assumption, or with independent uniform nonzero active CRT coordinates when that different law is explicitly assumed. Draw V0 uniform I independently conditional on T. Independently draw coefficient F~D_gamma^n and H00~D_eta^n. A conditioned finite-box Gaussian F is a separately specified variant. Publish the base pair

    (A,C0)=(U+F,V0+H00) mod q.

Independently sample L and W from their fixed public laws and publish (A,C)=(A,C0+LA+W). No binary secret or later G is available to this source algorithm. Writing V=V0+LU and H=H00+LF+W gives the exact SC0 form. Translation of V0 shows V remains uniform I and independent of (U,F,H) conditional on T. For every fixed A,L,W, C0->C0+LA+W is a permutation, so a uniform pair remains uniform. For any test D the public base-source test that samples L,W and runs D on this transform has exactly the same source advantage. Finite-precision errors add by data processing; the transform preserves the base U law and supplies no unit-conditioning reduction.

Here D_w(z)=exp(-pi z^2/w^2)/Z_w on the integers, independently coefficientwise when a superscript n is used. The tested Gaussian family lets L have independent centered coefficient Gaussian law D_kappa (its negative has the same law), including the deterministic L=0 limit. W may be any fixed independent law, including iid Gaussian broadening D_omega; H00's eta is arbitrary and may be smaller than the target width. The principal requested case is L=-G' with G' an independent copy of G~D_tau^n. The later bridge noise is then

    N=FG+H=H00+W+F(G-G') mod q.

All widths gamma,eta,kappa,omega,tau,sigma are public fixed parameters; none depends on hidden F. All statements below are about the exact wrapped-discrete experiment. H is integrated out, while J,U,F are retained as required by SC0.

## D02-B1 — a finite character obstruction, including smaller fixed H00

Define phi_w(theta)=E_(Z~D_w) exp(2pi i theta Z), and use its positive real Gaussian characteristic. Let p0=Pr[F0=0] and p1=Pr[F0=1]. Assume F0 is independent of the joint tuple of the other F coefficients and (J,U). Assume G and L are mutually independent, independent of (J,U,F), and have the stated independent coefficient laws. Conditional on J,U, the variable X=H00+W is independent of the entire tuple (F,G,L). Let

    eta_noise=TV((J,U,F,N),(J,U,F,E)),
    E~D_sigma^n mod q independently of (J,U,F).

H is integrated out. In the principal L=-G' case, for every integer k,

    eta_noise >= (min(p0,p1)/2) phi_sigma(k/q)
                    * (1-phi_tau(k/q)^2).                       (B1)

The same conclusion for independent iid Gaussian L of width kappa replaces phi_tau^2 by phi_tau*phi_kappa. For L=0 it replaces it by phi_tau. Thus the lower bound is independent of eta and of the amount of independent Gaussian broadening W. Shrinking the base second-noise width cannot remove it.

**Proof.** Condition on all F coefficients except F0, and also on J,U. At the coefficient-zero character exp(2pi i k N0/q), all other factors can be denoted by one complex number x when F0=0. When F0=1 the characteristic is z x, where z=phi_tau(k/q)^2 in the principal case. This uses the negacyclic expansion N0=F0(G0-G0')-sum_(j>=1) F_j(G_(n-j)-G'_(n-j))+(H00+W)0 and the independent coefficient laws. The target coefficient characteristic is the same positive number b=phi_sigma(k/q) on both branches.

For every complex x and real z in [0,1],

    b(1-z)=|z(x-b)-(zx-b)| <= |x-b|+|zx-b|.

The conditional total-variation distances epsilon_0,epsilon_1 on the two branches obey 2epsilon_0>=|x-b| and 2epsilon_1>=|zx-b|, because a unit-modulus test has expectation discrepancy at most twice TV. Consequently p0 epsilon_0+p1 epsilon_1>=min(p0,p1)b(1-z)/2. Contributions from all other F0 values are nonnegative. Averaging the retained conditioning variables proves (B1). Gaussian broadening only changes x and therefore cannot evade this inequality. The argument never treats modular sums as unwrapped sums and uses no covariance approximation.

If iid coefficients of L have a different fixed law, factorization still gives z=phi_tau(theta)*E exp(2pi i theta L0), possibly complex. The inequality becomes b|1-z|<=|x-b|+|zx-b| since |z|<=1, and |1-z|>=1-|z|>=1-phi_tau(theta). Thus an analogous lower bound holds for arbitrary *iid* public shear coefficients. Correlated-coefficient L is not covered by that factorization; no general nonlinear/public-simulator impossibility is asserted.

## Explicit lower bound at the common candidate widths

Let ell=ceil(log2 n)>=1, gamma=tau=ell, sigma=2n ell^3, and suppose q>=8sigma. This last condition holds eventually along the frozen q/sigma>=n^c baseline. Choose

    k=floor(q/(4sigma)),   theta=k/q,
    1/(8sigma)<=theta<=1/(4sigma).

Then b=phi_sigma(theta)>=1-pi sigma^2 theta^2>=1-pi/16=:b0>0. The variance inequality used here is exact for the integer Gaussian: Var(D_sigma)<=sigma^2/(2pi).

The scalar normalizer satisfies Z_ell<=1+ell. For every integer 1<=j<=ell, D_ell(j)>=exp(-pi)/(1+ell). Since j theta<=1/2, the elementary inequality 1-cos(2pi x)>=8x^2 gives

    1-phi_ell(theta)
      >=16 theta^2 sum_(j=1)^ell j^2 D_ell(j)
      >=(16/3) exp(-pi) ell^2 theta^2.

Also min(p0,p1)=D_ell(1)>=exp(-pi)/(1+ell), and conditioning F0 on |F0|<=ell^2 only increases these probabilities. Since 1-phi_ell(theta)^2>=1-phi_ell(theta), (B1) yields

    eta_noise >= b0 exp(-2pi) ell^2 / (24(1+ell) sigma^2)
              = b0 exp(-2pi) / (96 n^2 ell^4(1+ell)).             (B2)

The same lower bound holds for every independent iid Gaussian L width kappa, because 1-phi_tau*phi_kappa>=1-phi_tau, including L=0. It also holds under the iid-law extension just stated. This is inverse polynomial, not negligible. For an implementation whose complete joint sampling law is within eta_impl of the ideal experiment, the lower bound becomes max(0,right side of B2-eta_impl). Negligible implementation errors do not remove it.

This lower bound concerns the *strong retained-F noise condition*. It does not by itself distinguish the public source pair, because the branch variable F0 is hidden from a source adversary. It does not prohibit a finite advantage statement restricted to distinguishers with advantage larger than the actual simulation error. It rules out the proposed negligible-TV transfer for every nonnegligible adversarial advantage under a fixed polynomial parameter schedule. Candidate A is explicitly outside the lemma: H|F is a different anisotropic kernel on the F0=0 and F0=1 fibers, so there is no common x with the asserted multiplicative relation.

For the general polynomial-width reading, put s=max(sigma,1), assume q>=8s, and choose k=floor(q/(4s)). If tau>=tau0>0, the monotonicity phi_tau<=phi_tau0 established in F02-N1 gives

    eta_noise >= (1-pi/16) min(p0,p1) D_tau0(1)/(8s^2).          (B3)

Indeed theta lies in [1/(8s),1/(4s)], the target characteristic is at least 1-pi/16, and 1-phi_tau(theta)>=16 D_tau0(1)theta^2. For gamma in [gamma0,poly(n)], p1=D_gamma(1)>=exp(-pi/gamma0^2)/(1+gamma), so B3 is inverse polynomial whenever sigma is polynomial. This consequence uses the already recorded theta-product monotonicity lemma; B2 independently proves the main ell-width case without that import.

## Exact bridge accounting and common inequalities

For a one-sample decision test D, the frozen bridge, when its independent source and extraction hypotheses hold, has the schematic finite bound

    Adv_target(D) <= Adv_base(T1)+Adv_base(T2)
                    +eta_ext+eta_noise+eta_iso+eta_sampling.   (B4)

T1,T2 are the respective public source reductions composed with the explicit shear above; each composition preserves its distinguishing advantage exactly. The extraction and noise terms must use the same source law and independently sampled G. Any implementation distances not already included in eta_noise or eta_ext enter eta_sampling once. A lower bound on eta_noise does not provide a useful upper bound for B4; it proves that the proposed all-negligible error budget cannot be met in this family.

For active-unit U, eta_iso=0. For the nearest original HLBDD law U uniform I, the failure of the ideal isomorphism gives the exact averaged comparison cost

    eta_iso=1-(1-1/q+1/q^2)^d.

This is distinct from changing the source to condition on active units, whose conditioning distance is 1-(1-1/q)^d. Neither is negligible for the stated fixed polynomial parameter schedules. The public shear preserves the actual U law and supplies neither reduction for free. Likewise, a base source with eta different from the original HLBDD Gaussian width is a separately specified source unless an additional reduction justifies it. D02-B1 grants arbitrary eta only to strengthen the completion obstruction, not to claim all these base sources inherit the same hardness assumption.
