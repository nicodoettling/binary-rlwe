# S10 — Independent source assumptions: two first samplers

**Outcome:** neither checked source family supplies an independent hardness justification for the reference target. One transport has a rigorous quantitative oracle reduction, but its required source is essentially a nearby RLWE assumption. The other loses recovery information before reaching the target modulus.

## 1. Planted quasi-cyclic syndrome decoding

**Classification: established/independently studied assumption, in its own parameter regimes. Source-verified result:** systematic index-two QCSD samples uniform \(h\in\mathbb F_2[X]/(X^d-1)\), independent weight-\(w\) binary \(x,y\), and publishes \(z=x+hy\). Its source paper distinguishes planted generation from decision against uniform syndromes and explicitly does not claim a general complexity theorem for QC decoding. [Aguilar et al., Definitions 7, 11–13 and Assumption 1, PDF pp. 8–11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05572).

**First sampler; fully written small calculation:** choose uniform binary \(T\), set \(S=y\oplus T\) implicitly, and output \((h,B=z+hT)\). Over characteristic two, \(B=hS+x\), with iid uniform binary \(S\); recovery gives \(y=S\oplus T\), then \(x=z+hy\). Thus the first arrow is planted QCSD \(\leq\) uniform-secret, fixed-weight-noise binary-field ring recovery. Unlike lossiness, this attempts to transport the planted witness directly.

**Serious objection:** conditional on unit \(h\), this one-sample \(B\) is uniform already. Given \((h,B)\), all \(\binom d w\) noise choices give equally likely secrets, so planted recovery probability is at most \(\binom{d}{w}^{-1}\). Publishing \(T\) adds leakage. The apparently successful uniformization therefore creates an information-theoretic recovery obstruction.

The proposed next arrow to odd-\(q\) RLWE is **unresolved**. With coefficient lifts, \(\widetilde z=\widetilde h\widetilde y+\widetilde x-2K\), where the unknown integer carry \(K\) depends on the witness. There is no nonzero additive homomorphism from this characteristic-two ring to an odd-characteristic ring. Adding independent uniform even masks makes the output noise uniform because 2 is invertible.

**Ledger:** source degree \(d\) is not automatically a growing power of two; cyclic ring, modulus 2, coefficient basis, two hidden sparse blocks, one ring equation; masked channel has rank one and one sample, but fixed-weight error. For power-of-two \(d\), \(X^d-1=(X+1)^d\). No Gaussian-noise or additional-independent-sample conversion follows. **Cheap diagnostic:** test any proposed lift on \(d=2,h=1+X,x=1,y\in\{1,X\}\): both source syndromes equal \(X\), but their integer carries differ.

## 2. Planted two-block compact knapsack

**Classification:** compact knapsack is independently studied; assuming hardness of the exact two-binary-block corner below is **tailored**, absent a separate source-regime bridge. Published results use other widths/block counts; the negacyclic collision theorem requires compression, including \(k>\log q/\log(2d)\). [Micciancio, Section 4.1](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~daniele/papers/Cyclic.pdf); [Lyubashevsky–Micciancio, Theorem 5.1](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~daniele/papers/IdealHash.pdf).

**Actual arrow and sampler; fully written small calculation:** sample uniform \(a_1,a_2\in R_q\), iid binary \(x,y\), and publish \(t=a_1x+a_2y\). For unit \(a_2\), output
\[
A=a_2^{-1}a_1,\qquad B=a_2^{-1}t+G=Ax+y+G,
\]
with iid coefficient \(G_j\sim D_\sigma\). Then \(A\) is uniform, and recovered \(x\) gives \(y=a_2^{-1}t-Ax\), which is verified directly.

For target one-sample success \(\epsilon\), discrete-Gaussian likelihood comparison and Cauchy–Schwarz give conditional source success at least
\[
\epsilon^2\left((1+e^{2\pi/\sigma^2})/2\right)^{-n}.
\]
The comparison survives wrapping modulo \(q\); no negligible-TV assertion is needed. It preserves nonnegligible success when \(n/\sigma^2=O(\log n)\). For odd prime \(q>2n\), nonunit abort probability is at most \(n/q\).

**Ledger:** target rank one, power-of-two degree \(n\), coefficient basis, iid binary secret, odd prime polynomial modulus, one ring sample; Gaussian width must also satisfy the reference \(q/\sigma\) constraint. Rotations provide no independent samples.

**Missing implication/objection:** established compact-knapsack hardness \(\to\) this two-block source is unproved here. Normalization exposes binary-secret/binary-error RLWE. A uniform target lies in its planted support with probability at most \((4/q)^n\), so uniform-target and planted hardness cannot be interchanged. **Cheap next diagnostic:** reject proposed bridges that ignore this support bound or require more ring blocks. Details and source-access records are in `S10/calculations.md` and `S10/source-notes.md`; no experiments were run.
