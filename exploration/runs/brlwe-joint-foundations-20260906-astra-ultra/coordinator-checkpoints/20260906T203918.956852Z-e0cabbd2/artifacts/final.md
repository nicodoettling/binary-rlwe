# Partial research report — review in progress

The exact correlated computational premise **P0-A2 remains unsupported**. No polynomial-time distinguisher for the provided one-pair source was obtained. The finite investigation has produced three types of scoped results, whose author proofs are frozen and undergoing review.

1. **Source conversion limits (selected direction W1).** A public map broadens the second noise of a precisely stated small-noise HLBDD pair, with explicit Gaussian and truncation error, but preserves its uniform-in-ideal first point. For the broad independently noised active-point source, a discrete entropy argument proposes an inverse-polynomial obstruction to producing A2 with any stochastic map that preserves the uniform pair exactly. This is restricted to c>1/2 and does not cover merely approximate comparator preservation or the original small-noise input. A separate conditional argument shows that a repeatable A-only sampler for the hidden completion kernel would permit a polynomial autocorrelation distinguisher. That sampler is an additional capability, not part of the source challenge.

2. **Observable alternative (selected direction W3).** A full hybrid uses the independently noised pair (U+F,V+E_sigma), leaving H_seq only in the proof. Its finite advantage cost is two source advantages plus 2 eta_N+eta_X+eta_iso, where eta_iso=E[1-|UR|/|I|]. For active U this last term is zero; source hardness for that active-point law remains unsupported. For uniform U, eta_iso=1-(1-1/q+1/q^2)^d. An explicit exponential public test proposes a matching statistical lower bound eta_iso-negl for the stated endpoint comparison when c>1/2. It is not a PPT source attack.

3. **Structural triage (W2, no further development selected).** Empty-orbit counting improves sufficient constants in the existing exp(O(d)) attack, while a union over all small-index subgroups bounds the coverage of this method at degree o(d). Scalar shears do not cancel a fixed fraction of the ideal support with nonnegligible probability. A known-support PV search problem reduces to first-component recovery, with a positive-event active-conditioning factor; this does not supply decisional P0 hardness or an exact-pair simulator.

The common regime is n a sufficiently large power of two, ell=ceil(log2 n), d=floor(sqrt(n)/ell^4), b=ell^2, tau=ell, sigma=2n ell^3, and split prime sigma*n^c<=q<=n^K for fixed c,K. The shared example c=1,K=16 uses the inherited prime-existence result and lies in the c>1/2 subregime of the entropy/rank obstructions. All work has m=1. No common-K or multi-sample gate was opened.
