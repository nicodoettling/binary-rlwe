# F03 — exact paired noisy-ideal source and primary-source compatibility

Foundation completed 2026-09-06, before the 15:10 UTC task deadline. Investigator status: source compatibility assessed; new mathematical deductions remain provisional pending nonauthor review. No hardness theorem or novelty claim. No experiment allocated or run.

## Assessment

The independently studied nearest source is Order-LWE Section 7's HLBDD pair: two fresh uniform points in one uniformly selected CRT-support ideal, independently perturbed by discrete Gaussians. The exact source required by SC0 additionally conditions its first point to be a unit on the active support and permits an F-dependent completion law for H. Those changes must be charged or separately assumed. The published entropic reduction has noise-swallowing and active-invertibility steps outside the frozen polynomial-q baseline. DSPR and partial Vandermonde problems are useful comparisons, but neither presently supplies this exact joint law.

## Frozen interface and exact experiment

Let n be a power of two, q a prime congruent to 1 modulo 2n, and fix an ordered list of roots rho_1,...,rho_n of X^n+1 in F_q. Let ev:R_q -> F_q^n be their CRT evaluation isomorphism. Public parameters include n,q,d, the root list, a support sampler, all perturbation-law parameters, a named conditional kernel K, and a finite-precision budget. There is no binary secret in the sampler input or output.

1. Draw a support T uniformly among the d-subsets of [n]. Define J_T={x:ev(x)_i=0 for i in T}; I_T=Ann(J_T)={x:ev(x)_i=0 for i outside T}. Thus |R_q/J_T|=|I_T|=q^d. Both ideals are hidden.
2. Draw independently u_i uniform in F_q^* for i in T and put u_i=0 otherwise. Set U=ev^{-1}(u). This active-unit law gives an isomorphism x mod J_T -> Ux from R_q/J_T to I_T.
3. Draw F according to its explicitly specified law, and draw H from K(n,q,d,T,U,F;fresh coins). K must not access S or G. This permits dependence on T,U,F; it does not assert that F and H are independent.
4. Independently conditional on T, draw v_i uniform in F_q for i in T and zero otherwise. Set V=ev^{-1}(v). In particular V is independent of (U,F,H) given T.
5. Publish only (A,C)=(U+F,V+H) modulo q. Compare with (A_0,C_0) sampled independently and uniformly from R_q.

The source assumption is: every PPT B has negligible absolute difference between its acceptance probabilities on those two experiments, for this fixed asymptotic parameter and kernel family. Writing K as a parameter does not establish a concrete sampler or its hardness: a candidate must instantiate it. The independent-G bridge, extraction, and FG+H noise condition are additional, separate claims.

Two law specifications useful for comparison (not two admitted development candidates):

* Independent Gaussian reference: F~D_gamma^n and H~D_eta^n, independently of each other and T,U,V; all coefficients then wrap modulo q. If eta=gamma and U is changed to uniform I_T, this is exactly the intended two-sample HLBDD distribution in the coefficient normalization of a power-of-two cyclotomic.
* Completion interface: F~D_gamma^n (or an explicitly conditioned/truncated version) and H|T,U,F~K_{T,U,F}; sigma is the target width, tau the separately fixed G width. These four widths are distinct. Merely writing H=E-FG is not an admissible kernel because the source challenger has no G from the later bridge experiment. A continuous covariance complement is a diagnostic kernel, not yet an exact wrapped-discrete construction.

Uniform subset sampling and CRT inversion take polynomial time. Nonzero active values can be sampled directly, avoiding global rejection. If one instead draws U uniform in I_T and rejects any active zero, the acceptance probability is (1-1/q)^d and expected trials are its inverse; q>=2n+1 and d<=n give a constant bound. Nevertheless the exact changed mass delta_unit=1-(1-1/q)^d is generally inverse polynomial, not negligible. Public data processing gives TV at most delta_unit; it does not make that loss negligible. For polynomial widths, truncating D_w to |z|<=ceil(w*n), approximating weights with polynomially many bits, and then reducing modulo q gives a PPT implementation with negligible joint sampling error. Any additional conditional rejection or K approximation needs its own acceptance and precision bound.

## An exact source reduction for a limited correlated family

Fix coefficient widths gamma,beta,kappa>0, or replace the kappa law below by a specified deterministic polynomial l. The **base experiment** uses the same T and active-unit U law as above, with independent F~D_gamma^n, H_0~D_beta^n and V_0 uniform I_T. The base challenge is (A,C_0)=(U+F,V_0+H_0). The **sheared experiment** additionally samples independent L~D_kappa^n and publishes

    (A,C)=(A,C_0+L A).

All arithmetic is modulo q and all Gaussian laws are coefficient discrete Gaussians before wrapping. This is a fully specified PPT source law at the previously stated precision budget; the shear width kappa, base second-noise width beta, first-noise width gamma, later auxiliary width tau, and target width sigma are distinct parameters.

Set V=V_0+LU and H=H_0+LF. Conditional on (T,U,F,H_0,L), translation by LU permutes I_T, so V is uniform in I_T and independent of that whole tuple given T. In particular V is independent of (U,F,H) given T. Thus the sheared experiment satisfies the exact SC0 source interface and its conditional completion law is

    H | F=f  ~  W_beta,q^n * (M_f)_* W_kappa,q^n,

where M_f is negacyclic multiplication by f and convolution is on the additive group R_q. For deterministic l this becomes the translate lf+W_beta,q^n. There is no binary secret or later G in the source sampler.

**Finite computational reduction.** Given any sheared-source test D, define B_D(a,c) by sampling fresh L from the stated law and returning D(a,c+La). This is public and needs no hidden source tuple. For every fixed a,L, translation maps a uniform c to uniform c+La. Hence the transformation sends a uniform pair to a uniform pair, and

    Adv_sheared(D) = Adv_base(B_D)

exactly, before explicitly charged implementation errors. This is a genuine source-level hardness transfer, not a new hardness assumption for the shear. It does not remove the base experiment's active-unit versus uniform-U mismatch with HLBDD. Starting from unconditioned HLBDD yields the same unconditioned U after the shear, so that mismatch still costs delta_unit or requires another theorem.

**Completion limitation.** Let v_w be the exact variance of the integer D_w law. For unwrapped independent centered G~D_tau^n, L~D_kappa^n and H_0~D_beta^n, conditioning on F gives

    Cov(FG+H | F)
      = (v_tau+v_kappa) M_F M_F^T + v_beta I.

The continuous-Gaussian analogue replaces v_w by w^2/(2pi). The shear adds a positive semidefinite term. In particular L=-G' for a fresh independent copy G' gives H=H_0-FG' and response noise H_0+F(G-G'), not a cancellation of the actual G. This covariance statement is an unwrapped diagnostic; it does not rule out all wrapped parameter regimes or nonlinear public transformations. Whether a *source-inherited* correlation can supply the desired wrapped-discrete completion is the proper second-slot development question.

## Compatibility table

| Requirement | Verified status at the same baseline parameters |
|---|---|
| Exact efficiently sampled hidden support, active U, independent V | Available as the sampler above; active conditioning has constant expected cost but nonnegligible law change from uniform ideal U. |
| Rich support extraction with Y retained | Not established by the source literature. Existing ring-LHL identities must be evaluated for this exact support/leakage law. Basic capacity requires d log_2 q<=n up to the desired statistical slack. |
| Independent Gaussian source hardness | HLBDD is an existing nonstandard assumption for its precise independent-noise/unconditioned-U law. It is not established from worst-case Order-LWE. |
| F-dependent covariance-complement source hardness | No matching primary-source reduction identified. Efficient samplability or correct completion does not establish this computational claim. At present it is a tailored hypothesis. |
| Public-shear correlation hardness | Inherits the exact base source by the finite reduction above. It supplies one justified correlation family but presently no useful completion improvement. |
| Old Order-LWE entropic reduction at polynomial q | Its negligible swallowing ratios force negligible source perturbation when target widths are polynomial, which makes a proper-ideal source efficiently distinguishable. Its active-invertibility step also uses superpolynomial q. |
| DSPR/NTRU source replacement | A real common-denominator tuple reduction exists, with separate DSPR and RLWE assumptions. The available entropy/noise/sample endpoint does not match this binary-secret paired source. |
| Rich-support attacks | Binomial controls remain broken. The new subgroup-super-support attack runs in exp(O(d)) under the explicit narrow-F margin; this rules out d=O(log n) there but does not prove anything about hardness when d=n/polylog(n). |
| Polynomially many target samples or planted search recovery | No implication supplied by F03. The old rerandomization needs its own assumptions and widths; shared V_i=U_iK is not independent ideal sampling. |

The gate can therefore admit a rich-support completion family only with its exact source-pseudorandomness statement marked unresolved, and a public-shear family as a substantively different source-inherited correlation to test. No nonempty polynomial-q regime with *all* extraction, completion, source hardness and intended task requirements is currently supported by an exact literature theorem. This is a gap assessment, not a general impossibility statement and not a hardness inference from failed attacks.

## Evidence, strongest objection, and next test

The full [source/version ledger](F03-source-ledger.md) records the exact theorem/definition numbers, source normalization and sample conditions. The [attack note](F03-attacks.md) gives support enumeration, code decoding controls, subgroup-super-support decoding, short multipliers, pair combinations and a conditional Fourier identity. Local primary text copies were used for the supplied PDFs. A local pdftotext convenience command was unavailable; this did not require an installation or change any conclusion.

The strongest objection is the missing computational justification for the exact F-conditioned completion source. Marginal HLBDD hardness, a continuous covariance identity, and an efficient anisotropic discrete sampler cannot be combined into that justification. The strongest useful attack refinement is A2: at q^(1-d/r)>=8 n^(1+epsilon)b, a hidden support of size d can be covered by at most d of r subgroup orbits and decoded after exp(O(d)) enumeration.

The next discriminating mathematical test is whether an explicit public map of the independent-noise base pair can realize a completion law satisfying the bridge at the same widths. Public shears give a correct source map to start from, but their elementary additive form does not create the negative covariance complement. If that family fails, preserve the exact scope of the failure; an arbitrary new H kernel should remain a separate tailored hypothesis.
