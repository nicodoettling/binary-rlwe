## D03-Y1: separate observations require a different joint extractor and source

Suppose Y_i=S+G_i, with iid G_i~D_tau^n independent of the entire source. The public reduction can sample the joint law of (Y_i)_i by sampling one fresh binary S_0 and all G_i. This algorithmic fact does not make the quotient secrets independent.

For one common ideal J, the object being extracted is the SINGLE Z_real=pi_J(S), conditioned on the FULL vector Y_vec and every retained source variable. If that object is replaced by one uniform Z, the resulting ideal points are

    V_i=U_i K_i,    K_i=Z-pi_J(Y_i),
    K_i-K_j=pi_J(Y_j-Y_i).                                     (Y1)

Conditional on Y_vec, the K vector lies on a translated diagonal; its translate depends on Y_vec. It is therefore neither an independent vector of uniform quotient secrets nor the independent common-K source of D03-common-k.md. In general the source challenge cannot generate this Y-dependent conditional law independently of the reduction's observations. The public map knows A_i and Y_i, not hidden U_i, so it cannot simply add the necessary U_i*pi_J(Y_j-Y_i) corrections. A different public source interface or a complete public-law identity is required.

Trying instead to extract (pi_J(S),...,pi_J(S)) into m independent quotient variables fails even before leakage is considered: the real tuple is on the diagonal. Against the independent uniform product its TV is at least 1-|Q|^(1-m), using the equality event. This argument does not depend on the entropy of S.

If ideals J_i vary with fully split active supports T_i, the tuple of projections must agree on all repeated CRT coordinates. Its uniform linear-image dimension is |union_i T_i|, whereas an independent quotient product has dimension sum_i |T_i|. The overlap-consistency event gives distance at least 1-q^(|union T_i|-sum |T_i|) when comparing a real-consistent tuple to that independent product. Even with disjoint supports, extraction given all Y_i must supply the entire output entropy, and the observations still concern one binary S. Such a change would require a separately specified source and full hybrid; it is not developed as a third candidate here.

## D03-Y2: exact likelihood and an information budget

First consider unwrapped observations of a scalar fair bit B:

    Y_i=B+G_i,     G_i~D_tau independently,     1<=i<=m.

At each integer observation vector y, the exact likelihood ratio is

    Pr[Y_vec=y|B=1]/Pr[Y_vec=y|B=0]
      = exp((pi/tau^2)(2sum_i y_i-m)).                           (Y2)

Thus sum_i Y_i is a sufficient statistic, the MAP rule thresholds the average at 1/2, and the posterior is logistic with the exponent in Y2. In the n-coefficient model the prior and all observation kernels factor over coefficients, so the posterior also factors over coefficients after the ENTIRE observation vector is fixed. An exact scalar expression for conditional entropy is therefore

    H(S|Y_vec)=n E[h_2(logistic((pi/tau^2)(2sum_i Y_i-m)))],      (Y3)

where h_2 is binary entropy in bits and the expectation is under the scalar mixture experiment. This formula is for unwrapped observations; wrapped observations use the ratio of wrapped masses instead.

There is also a simple quantitative lower bound on residual entropy, valid after wrapping. The two scalar shifted-D_tau laws have KL divergences in BOTH directions equal to pi/tau^2 in natural units: expand log(D_tau(z)/D_tau(z-1)) and use mean zero. For m observations the divergence multiplies by m. Convexity in the second KL argument gives, for the fair mixture,

    I(B;Y_vec) <= pi m/(2tau^2) nats.

Wrapping contracts KL and mutual information. Because coefficient channels are independent,

    I(S;Y_vec mod q) <= n min(1, pi m/(2tau^2 ln 2)) bits,
    H(S|Y_vec mod q) >= n max(0,1-pi m/(2tau^2 ln 2)).           (Y4)

This bounds information LOSS from above when m is small. It does not prove that any particular quotient is uniform. In particular m=o(tau^2) leaves n-o(n) Shannon bits, but extraction still needs a distribution-specific theorem.

## D03-Y3: sufficiently many observations recover the auxiliary secret

The discrete Gaussian satisfies the exact subgaussian MGF inequality

    E exp(t G) <= exp(tau^2 t^2/(4pi)),     t real.              (Y5)

Completing the square writes the left side as exp(tau^2 t^2/(4pi)) times rho_tau(Z-a)/rho_tau(Z), a=tau^2 t/(2pi). The ratio is at most one by the Poisson series, since every cosine term is at most its value at zero. Chernoff's bound for the sum of m independent G_i then gives

    Pr[sum_i G_i >= m/2] <= exp(-pi m/(4tau^2)),

and the symmetric lower-tail bound. Consequently the scalar MAP error is at most epsilon_bit=exp(-pi m/(4tau^2)); arbitrary ties can be resolved consistently without worsening this bound. A union bound gives exact recovery of all n bits with failure at most n epsilon_bit in the unwrapped experiment.

For odd q>=5, the decoder can first center each modular observation. If every |G_ij|<=(q-3)/2, centering recovers all unwrapped S_j+G_ij exactly. Another use of Y5 and a union bound gives the valid wrapped failure bound

    epsilon_all <= n exp(-pi m/(4tau^2)) + xi_wrap,
    xi_wrap <= 2nm exp(-pi(q-3)^2/(4tau^2)).                    (Y6)

The constants are conservative; no no-wrap assumption is silently imposed. Under candidate A's polynomial sample budget and q/tau growing polynomially, xi_wrap is negligible. Taking m/(tau^2 log n)->infinity makes epsilon_all negligible. At m=C tau^2 log n, the first term is n^(1-pi C/4), giving an explicit inverse-polynomial failure when C>4/pi. For tau=ceil(log2 n), the transition for full-secret recovery is at order log^3 n, with the displayed constants and error quantifiers. This is an attack on the exposed AUXILIARY observation collection, not an attack that reads hidden Y_i from an RLWE transcript.

It directly obstructs the strong extractor needed by this route. Given J,Y_vec compute S_hat by the preceding decoder and test whether the candidate quotient equals pi_J(S_hat). If the real quotient pi_J(S) is used, acceptance is at least 1-epsilon_all. If the quotient is fresh uniform independent of J,Y_vec and |Q|=q^d, acceptance is exactly q^(-d). Therefore

    eta_ext,separate >= 1-epsilon_all-q^(-d).                   (Y7)

For every nontrivial quotient d>=1, the proposed negligible-error extraction is impossible when epsilon_all is negligible. This conclusion concerns the explicitly retained leakage, not every possible multiple-sample reduction.

A further entropy necessary condition can be made quantitative. Let epsilon_j<=exp(-pi m/(4tau^2))+2m exp(-pi(q-3)^2/(4tau^2)) bound a single bit's modular decoding error, and cap it at 1/2. Binary Fano and coefficient factorization give H(S|Y_vec)<=n h_2(epsilon_j). If extraction at TV at most eta<=1/2 is claimed for a fixed quotient size q^d, conditional entropy continuity on the same (J,Y_vec) marginal yields

    (1-eta)d log2 q <= n h_2(epsilon_j)+h_2(eta).                (Y8)

Indeed H(pi_J(S)|J,Y_vec) must be at least d log2 q-eta log2(q^d-1)-h_2(eta), while it is at most H(S|Y_vec), since J is independent of S and its observations. This is necessary, not sufficient. For large leakage it strengthens the bare initial n-bit entropy budget.

For the ACTUAL extraction distance eta_actual, the coupling proof of conditional entropy continuity gives the useful bound at every distance

    eta_actual >= 1-[H(S|Y_vec)+1]/[d log2 q].                  (Y9)

To see this directly, couple the real quotient and the independent uniform quotient optimally conditionally on (J,Y_vec). The mismatch probability is eta_actual. Conditional Fano gives d log2 q<=H(pi_J(S)|J,Y_vec)+h_2(eta_actual)+eta_actual log2(q^d-1), and h_2<=1 yields Y9.

For A1's d=max(1,floor(n/ell^3)) and q between 2n+1 and n^K, d log2 q=Theta(n/log^2 n). If m>=C tau^2 ln ln n for any fixed C>8/pi, the bit error in Y6 is at most (ln n)^(-pi C/4) plus negligible wrapping error. Hence H(S|Y_vec)<=O(n (ln n)^(-pi C/4) ln ln n)=o(n/log^2 n). Equation Y9 forces eta_actual=1-o(1). Thus the initial quotient extractor already fails with about tau^2 log log n separate observations, before enough observations necessarily recover EVERY bit with overwhelming probability. This stronger threshold is for A1's output dimension; the full-S recovery threshold Y6 applies independently of d.

## D03-R: decision and recovery at an explicit sample budget

Let Rec receive exactly m target samples sharing S. Its exact planted success is epsilon_rec=Pr[Rec(transcript)=S]. Fix an efficiently decidable residual set T subset R, and write

    delta_T=Pr[E not in T],   alpha_T=1-delta_T,
    v_T=|T|/|R|.

**Same-budget self-verification.** Run Rec on all m inputs, reject a nonbinary output, and accept if every residual b_i-a_i*S_hat is in T. On planted success those residuals equal E_i, so the planted acceptance is at least epsilon_rec-m delta_T, by a union bound. Success can depend on the input errors; multiplying epsilon_rec by alpha_T^m would be unjustified.

Under the independent uniform reference law, fix all public multipliers. For EACH fixed binary s, all m responses lie in their residual translates with probability v_T^m. Any adaptively selected binary answer from Rec can pass only if at least one of the 2^n candidates passes. Thus the uniform acceptance is at most min(1,2^n v_T^m), without requiring Rec's answer to be independent of its input. It follows that the constructed decision test satisfies

    Adv_dec,m >= max(0,epsilon_rec-m delta_T-2^n v_T^m).         (R1)

Zero divisors in the multipliers cause no problem: the response is uniform for each fixed multiplier and candidate, so each residual translate has exactly |T| points. Verification detects a valid residual property; it does not certify that the answer is the original planted secret. For example over F_3, a=b=1 and T={0,1} let both binary candidates 0 and 1 pass, with respective residuals 1 and 0. Exact planted success is used only for the one-way lower bound.

**Held-out verification.** Suppose instead a decision test receives m+h independent samples and Rec sees only the first m. Condition on the complete prefix, Rec's tape, and S. On Rec's exact success, the h fresh errors have their iid target law pointwise under this conditioning. Thus simultaneous held-out verification has conditional probability alpha_T^h and planted acceptance at least epsilon_rec alpha_T^h. On a fully uniform challenge, condition on the complete prefix, candidate and all held-out multipliers. The h held-out responses remain independent uniform, so acceptance is at most v_T^h. Hence

    Adv_dec,m+h >= max(0,epsilon_rec alpha_T^h-v_T^h).           (R2)

There is no union over candidates in this null bound, because the candidate is fixed before the fresh responses. If Rec sees the held-out responses, or they are cached/rotated/shared rather than fresh, the pointwise conditional independence used here fails. R2 consumes m+h decision samples; R1 consumes m. These are separate valid calculations.

**Baseline specialization.** Take B=ceil(sigma log n) and T the coefficient residues represented in [-B,B]. With q/sigma>=n^c, q>=2n+1 and q<=n^K, eventually 2B+1<q and

    v_T=((2B+1)/q)^n,
    (2B+1)/q <= 2log(n)/n^c+3/q.

The Gaussian tail bound gives delta_T<=n sigma^2 exp(-pi log^2 n)/(pi B Z_sigma), which is negligible for polynomial sigma. Therefore for every fixed polynomial m>=1, both m delta_T and 2^n v_T^m are negligible. The latter is no larger than 2^n v_T once v_T<=1. A separately established negligible-advantage m-sample decision theorem would therefore rule out nonnegligible exact planted recovery using the SAME m samples. No held-out samples are necessary in this reference regime, including at m=1.

Combining R1 with K4, if its source and statistical hypotheses were established, would give

    epsilon_rec <= Adv_SrcK,m(B1_of_DRec)+Adv_SrcK,m(B2_of_DRec)
                   +eta_N,m+eta_X,m+m delta_T+2^n v_T^m,

plus the exact implementation terms. This is an average-success statement, not amplification. A merely inverse-polynomial or constant bound on decision advantage yields that corresponding finite upper bound on recovery; it cannot silently become negligible recovery. Published mild-search notions and unrestricted-oracle amplification do not establish the missing m-sample decision premise here.

## Existing sample expansion: checked interfaces and limits

The following are comparisons, not imports proving the frozen target.

**Order-LWE.** The primary full version of Bolboceanu–Brakerski–Perlman–Sharma, [Lemma 7.6, printed pp.39–40](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/494.pdf), starts from one sample (a_tilde,b_tilde) and outputs (a_tilde*z+e',b_tilde*z+e'') with fresh Gaussian z,e',e''. Its target error is e_tilde*z+e''-e'*S. The lemma assumes additional ordinary RLWE hardness, changes widths, and imposes Definition 2.13's negligible swallowing ratios; it is not repetition of the one-sample hybrid. The paired outputs share z, so any import must use a joint RLWE indistinguishability argument, not just the two marginal statements in the abbreviated proof. The exact parameter ledger is F03-source-ledger.md. At candidate A's polynomial seed and output widths those negligible separations are not established; this route supplies no unchanged polynomial-q Gaussian sample expansion. The neighboring one-sample HLBDD step also uses superpolynomial q for active invertibility. No broader audit or refutation of the published theorem is asserted.

**Brakerski–Döttling.** In the supplied primary [2020/1185](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185), Section 3.1, Lemma 3.5 explicitly uses subset sums of k original samples. In the paper's notation the condition is k>=log|G|+n log q+omega(log lambda). Given the initial errors e_1,...,e_k, each new error is sum_i x_i e_i for fresh uniform binary x. Its conditional error law is the random distribution chi chosen by the initial errors; it is not the fixed iid D_sigma law. For B-bounded original errors the stated bound grows to kB. The lemma concerns mild search hardness; Lemma 3.4's amplification additionally uses a fresh-sample oracle. Reading the primary text confirms the artifact rather than removing it. Source dimensions and secret entropy requirements also differ, as recorded in F03's ledger. None of these facts establishes the present fixed-sample, unchanged-error decisional conclusion.

Rotations, repeated added noise at one multiplier, and fresh source instances with independent secrets do not provide independent target samples sharing the planted S. Multiplying a single pair by public ring elements preserves algebraic dependencies; additional noise would need the full conditional error and public-comparator argument. No supported expansion from A1 has been supplied in this assignment.

## Mandatory finite controls

The shared-K F_3 diagonal control, with TV 2/3 from independent ideal points, is calculated in D03-common-k.md. It tests the exact source image and does not promote retained distance to public distance.

For the Gaussian-sign control, let S,T be independent fair bits, Z~D_sigma independent, and fixed public multipliers A_1=A_2=0. Set E_1=Z and E_2=(-1)^(S xor T)Z modulo odd q. Conditional on every S,T, EACH error marginal is exactly wrapped D_sigma. Without T, the pair is independent of S. Given T and a nonzero observed Z residue, equality versus negation of the two errors reveals S; at zero the bit remains fair. Hence p_guess(S|E_1,E_2,T)=1-W_sigma,q(0)/2, while p_guess(S|E_1,E_2)=1/2. Even unconditional covariance vanishes by sign symmetry in the unwrapped version, yet the errors are not a product Gaussian. This is a diagnostic violating the target joint kernel, not a target instance.

For the distinction between self-verification and held-out verification, take a two-atom probability space where solver success occurs exactly on the bad-error atom. Both events have probability 1/2, and their intersection with good error has probability zero. The incorrect same-input product epsilon_rec alpha_T would be 1/4; the valid subtraction epsilon_rec-delta_T is zero. Held-out multiplication is justified only by the explicit fresh conditional kernel in R2, so the imported cached-prefix regression cannot be bypassed by relabeling samples.

All controls are hand calculations; no experiment was allocated or executed.
