## Extraction audit and source check

Bayes' rule gives independent posterior bits with `p_y=W(y-1)/(W(y)+W(y-1))`. Averaging their squared Fourier factors gives exactly `1-beta*sin^2`, where `beta=4 E[p_Y(1-p_Y)]`. This averaging is justified by independence of the original coordinate pairs `(S_j,G_j)`. It would fail after an arbitrary mixed RLWE transcript were substituted for Y. The Gaussian law is wrapped throughout; no unwrapped tail heuristic enters the identity.

The inequality `beta>=1-D_tau(0)>=1-D_tau0(0)>0` follows from harmonic-mean versus minimum, contraction of TV under wrapping, and telescoping the unwrapped symmetric unimodal law. It is uniform even when tau grows. With `p=(1-sqrt(1-beta))/2`, the auxiliary Bernoulli law has precisely the required squared characteristic function and `1-||mu||_2^2=beta/2`. It changes no actual secret distribution.

I checked [Breuillard–Varju, Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09053), printed pp.3 and 7–9, including the normalization of T2 and the concluding Parseval estimate. The bound is unconditional and uniform in the prime, the prime-field step law, and every multiplier of order at least `C log q log log log q`. Its time bound is `C(1-||mu||_2^2)^(-1) log(q/delta) log q (log log q)^5`. The other theorems' typical-multiplier, fixed-integer-law, and GRH restrictions do not apply here.

For `delta=n^(-A-1)`, that time is `O(log^2 n*(log log n)^5)=o(log^3 n)` uniformly under the frozen polynomial q bound. The required multiplier order is eventually smaller than `2L`. The exact block factorization yields `(1+k_L)^d-1`, and `k_L<=delta^2` gives TV at most `sqrt(2d delta^2)/2<=n^(-A)` eventually. All constants may depend on the frozen positive lower width and block-size constants. There is no missing power of d or mistaken squared-accuracy parameter.

Power-of-two rounding does give an L in some fixed factor-two band around `log^3 n`, and eventually L divides n. It does not guarantee existence in every arbitrarily narrow preassigned band `[c_L log^3 n,C_L log^3 n]`. The dossier's theorem quantifies only over qualifying choices, so this is a wording qualification, not a refutation. Likewise the stated prime restriction does not establish nonemptiness for every fixed K or every n; no such claim is needed for its conditional theorem.

## Fatal sampler obstruction and the unsupported bridge

For `g=(X^n+1)/(X^d-a)`, every `U in (g)` has the unique block form

`U_(j+kd)=a^(L-1-k) P_j`.

For one block, enumerate `P_j in F_q` and test whether all L residuals lie in a known set E of size s. Planted acceptance is certain whenever F lies in that coefficient box. Uniform false acceptance is at most `q(s/q)^L`. All-block testing costs `O(qn)` field operations; hiding a among the L admissible roots adds only a polynomial factor and a union bound. Since `q/s>=n^c`, false acceptance remains negligible. This argument needs neither uniform P nor independence of P and F.

For Gaussian public perturbations, the dossier's `B=ceil(gamma log n)` cutoff and tail estimate are valid. Its polynomial prefactor is dominated by `exp(-pi log^2 n)`. The constraints `q/gamma>=n^c` and `q>=2n+1` ensure a polynomial gap between q and the interval size, including when gamma is small. No B response, secret recovery, or rotation-generated sample is needed for the attack.

The permissible statistical implication `(U,Y,US) approximately (U,Y,UZ_0)` follows by data processing only under the stated independence of U from `(S,Y)`. It does not simulate `(A,AS+E)` for uniform A. Writing `A=U+F` introduces `FS`; substituting Y requires an error-completion argument with the correct dependence on the public variables. The dossier contains no such argument for the target iid coefficient error law, even for one sample.

There is also a quantitative active-invertibility obstruction. For uniform U on its d active CRT coordinates,

`p_bad=1-(1-1/q)^d >= d/(2q)`.

Here `d/q<=1`, so the displayed lower bound applies. Under `q<=n^K`, this is at least an inverse-polynomial quantity of order `n^(1-K)/log^3 n`; it is not negligible in this regime. When U is exposed, replacing its uniform image output by an independent uniform element of the entire ideal incurs TV at least `(1-1/q)*p_bad`. The dossier flags this issue correctly, but “not automatically negligible” can be sharpened to “non-negligible under the stated bounds.”

The family in Definition 7.1 contains every product of k distinct prime factors; the surrounding prose requires every k-coordinate CRT marginal. Its displayed random-ideal definition averages over that full family. Either reading exceeds C01's L binomial supports. Definitions 7.3–7.4 and Lemma 7.7 use the noisy hidden-ideal decision problem, with two samples on the shared ideal in the reduction. Theorem 7.5 assumes ordinary RLWE as well. The swallowing ratio must be negligible, and hybrid H3 explicitly invokes super-polynomial q. The cited theorem is therefore not a worst-case justification for the tailored binomial source.

The coefficient target's polynomial parameter separations do not satisfy those swallowing conditions merely by changing Gaussian normalization. For this power-of-two coefficient basis the full canonical norm scales a scalar Gaussian width by `sqrt(n)`; that polynomial factor does not repair a required negligible ratio. No conclusion about a repaired general-ideal framework follows.

## Additional bounded calculation and its limitation

The coordinator's Fourier suggestion checks under an explicit frozen independent-completion model. For deterministic F and H independent of iid `G_tau`, TV epsilon from the target wrapped error law forces

\[
\max_k|\operatorname{center}_q(F_k)|^2
\le\frac{\pi\sigma^2+2q^2\epsilon}{16D_\tau(1)}.
\]

The proof uses positivity of the discrete Gaussian characteristic function and one output coordinate of the negacyclic product. With tau in a fixed compact positive interval and negligible TV jointly with sampler coins that preserve G's conditional iid law, every F coefficient is `O(sigma+1)` except with negligible coin probability. The existing decoder therefore excludes this additive-completion template without assuming F is small beforehand. The proof, exact coin quantifiers, and decoder corollary are in [additive-obstruction.md](C01/additive-obstruction.md).

Two limitations are essential. First, the finite bound's constant depends on `D_tau(1)`, so the written argument does not settle the original next lemma for polynomially growing tau. Second, correctness jointly with latent sampler coins is stronger than correctness of the reference public transcript. It cannot be imposed on every conceivable simulator without proof.

The loose phrase “H is independent of G conditional on sampler coins” also needs a formal repair. If `C=G`, arbitrary large F and `H=E-FG` satisfy that conditional-independence wording, and `(C,FG+H)` is exactly the independent target error law when E is fresh. The support note preserves this exact counterexample. The valid lemma requires that conditioning on C leaves the stated iid Gaussian law for G; for fixed tau, independent coins are sufficient.

## Scope of the diagnostic and remaining issue

Near-uniformity of the quotient is stronger than a residual-entropy statement. For this auxiliary channel,

`P_guess(S|Y) <= ((1+D_tau0(0))/2)^n`,

so average conditional min-entropy is already `Omega(n)`. Revealing any function with `d log_2 q=o(n)` output bits leaves `Omega(n)` average conditional min-entropy by the elementary leakage chain rule. C01's additional result is distributional uniformity of a specific quotient, potentially usable in a decision hybrid. It does not by itself improve a search-lossiness entropy budget, furnish a recovery map, or turn Y into the RLWE transcript. I found no explicit contrary claim in the dossier; its claimed difference from earlier work should be read at this limited distributional scope.

The exact remaining issue is an independently justified public sampler and simulator with the correct target transcript law and allowed noise, for which the quotient extraction is actually usable. The obvious sampler and the compact-width independent additive template are excluded as above. General supports, other leakage channels, dependence-aware completions, and the original broader tau range remain unproved here. Even a completed decision hybrid would still require a separate statement relating it to reference search hardness.
