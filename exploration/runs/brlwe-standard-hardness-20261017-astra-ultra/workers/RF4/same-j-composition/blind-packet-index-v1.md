# Same-ideal composition: mathematical statement packet

Read these three files as one mathematical packet:

1. same-j-theorem-only-v1.md — definitions, complete supplied-input assumptions, parameters, statement, and resource model.
2. interface-statements-v1.md — statements of the four mathematical interfaces and identifiers of their exact source statements/specifications.
3. same-j-specification-v1.md — composition algorithm definitions.

The composition question is whether the stated conclusion follows from the four explicit interfaces under precisely the supplied hypotheses, with the claimed law, normalizations, pointwise quantifiers, failure budget, and bounded circuit accounting. The interface statements themselves may also be assessed as stated mathematical dependencies. The two longer subroutine specifications are identified by exact paths and hashes in the interface file; their definitions are incorporated without a change of version.
