# Source significance at the delivered inherited radius

The actual available radius is r=epsilon_*ell^2/(1024 nK chi Qmax) in J^-1, with an ordinary integer basis of J and N(J)<=2^(rho n). There is no new full FF reduction and hence no new FF-delivered radius. The accepted source classifications below apply directly at this inherited radius, whose guards imply r<1/(4sqrt n). They are classical BDD algorithms, not an inference from an easy wide DGS sampler.

Acceptance-v1.json covers quotient-exponent rounding and the Frobenius prime family; acceptance-v3.json covers repaired symmetry descent and best-standard-subfield contraction. These supply substantive algorithmic classifications, including exponential-norm families and cases with trivial full Galois stabilizer. Their costs charge the numeric contraction index or characteristic when enumerated. The existence of a short algebraic element is never substituted for its acquisition algorithm.

Further source candidates have been independently reconstructed but their exact final gates are being reconciled. PRIME-SUPPORT-v2 extends the support condition through a weighted per-characteristic bound. ALL-SUBFIELDS-v1 shows that arbitrary-subfield binary-collision criteria offer the same polynomial-family criterion as the standard chain, provided the feasible set is nonempty. DENSE-SPLIT-v1 supplies eligible exponential-index, polynomial-characteristic ideals with trivial stabilizer and exponential feasible contraction indices. This diagnoses a limit of those collision methods; it does not classify the ideals as hard or even as resistant to other algorithms.

The direct RS, spectral-gap and list-recovery constructions address precisely that limitation. For consecutive split support,8p^2r^2<n-t+1 suffices for unique finite-field decoding; for arbitrary support the maximum spectral gap replaces n-t+1. List interpolation and polynomial-root recursion extend the consecutive case, with an explicit exact-lattice uniqueness check. ACTUAL-v3 gives a nonempty family with1/2<pr<=1 at a radius genuinely delivered by the inherited interface after a permitted charged chi enlargement. Scalar rounding's sufficient condition fails there, while RS decoding succeeds. The source norm and feasible subfield contraction can both be exponential. It does not assert that the same separation holds at the minimal chi=a radius.

A literal epsilon versus epsilon_* error in RS-v1's inherited-radius paragraph was found by the fresh blind reviewer after the full recommendation sealed. The full statement is rejected; its pointwise generic-r proof is separated. RS-v2 corrects the symbol. GAP/LIST/ACTUAL dependency gates are explicitly reopened, with fresh exact-version checks required. No earlier pass is silently inherited across this repair.

The remaining unclassified inputs include eligible ordinary-basis ideals outside all demonstrated effective acquisition and decoding guards, in particular unrestricted characteristic, prime-power and support patterns. Failed sufficient conditions, isolated easy examples, finite experiments and absence of an attack do not establish hardness. No effective compatible reduction from an independently established hardness assumption to this exact restricted source problem has been established. No new exact BDD-to-DGS composition is certified in this run; classical simulation of a wide DGS output would not reverse that implication into a BDD algorithm.
