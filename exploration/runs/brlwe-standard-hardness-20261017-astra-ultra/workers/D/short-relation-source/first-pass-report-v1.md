Three concrete candidates were checked:

A degree4/modulus17 symbolic counterexample survives modular periodization with an explicit rigorous tail bound. The uniform endpoint is exact. Retaining J=fR adds an unsimulated joint variable; publishing f makes the narrow source easy.
2. Uniform CRT ideals with d=Theta(n/log n), including proposed higher-index variants: new rank/support proof shows they contain ANY weight-O(log n) ternary relation only with probability exp(-Omega(n/log² n)). Always planting such a relation changes the ideal marginal by TV1-o(1). This excludes a statistical distribution substitution, not an arbitrary computational or class-changing reduction.

Arbitrary unknown support remains only quasipolynomially enumerable; no polynomial attack was established for that general family.
