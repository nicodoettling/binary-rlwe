# SV-ALL-SUBFIELDS-v1 author proof

For n>=4 write G=(Z/(2n)Z)^*=<-1> times<5>, with<5> cyclic of order n/2. This elementary structure follows because v2(5^(2^j)-1)=j+2 by repeated squaring, so5 generates all units1 modulo4; every odd unit is either1 or-1 modulo4. Let L be fixed by H and let g=[L:Q]=n/|H|. If g=n, L=K_n and the conclusion is immediate with m=n. Otherwise g<=n/2, m=2g.

Set h=|H|=n/g. Projection of H to G/<5>, a group of order2, shows |H intersect<5>|>=h/2. Since<5> is cyclic, H contains its unique subgroup of order h/2, namely<5^g>. The repeated-squaring valuation gives v2(5^g-1)=2+log2 g, so<5^g> is precisely the kernel of reduction G→(Z/(4g)Z)^*: both have order n/(2g). This includes g=1, for which the kernel is<5> and the fixed field is Q(i). The fixed field of this congruence kernel is the standard K_(2g), so reversing subgroup containment gives L⊂K_(2g). For n=2, the only subfields are Q and K_2, so the same conclusion follows directly. Thus[K_m:L] is1 or2 in all cases.

Let I=J_o intersect O_L. Because J_o is an ideal of R_n, multiplication by R_m preserves membership in J_o; hence

    I R_m ⊂ J_o intersect R_m.

For the extension of integer rings O_L⊂R_m of degree e'=[K_m:L], the ideal norm identity is

    [R_m:I R_m]=[O_L:I]^e'.

One proof factors I into prime ideals mathematically. For a prime P of O_L, the prime exponents and residue degrees above it have sum of products equal to e', so the absolute norm of P R_m is N_L(P)^e'; multiply over powers. No unramified assumption is required for this norm identity, and no factorization is used algorithmically. Lattice inclusion gives d_m<=[R_m:I R_m]=d_L^e'. Since m=e'g, d_L<2^g implies d_m<2^m. Minimizing proves d_*<=d_all^2, while the reverse inequality d_all<=d_* follows because standard subfields are among all subfields.

For effective enumeration, every subgroup H of C2 times C_(n/2) is generated by at most two elements: project to the cyclic second factor, choose a lift in H of a generator of its cyclic image, and adjoin a generator of the kernel, which lies in C2. The n^2 ordered pairs of automorphisms therefore include a generating pair for every H. Their closures and equality tests use polynomially many group operations on O(log n)-bit residues. The n=2 case is cyclic. Because K_n/Q is Galois and abelian, every subfield is the fixed field of one such subgroup. Signed-orbit consistency yields the full integral fixed lattice R_n^H=O_L, with disjoint signed-indicator supports, exactly as independently established in SV-SYMDESC-v2. Normal forms of J_o plus this lattice compute d_L with polynomial bit complexity. Enumeration does not bypass the numeric cost of d_L+1 collision candidates; its purpose is only to show that the comparison includes all available subfields effectively.

The result is a limitation of this specific contraction-and-binary-collision route. It does not claim equality of the minimizers, equality of finite costs, source hardness, or that every efficiently acquired short member arises from a binary collision in a subfield.
