## MR-v1 and FIXED-MAIN-v1 (provisional constructive alternative)

Scale larger-modulus ordinary binary RLWE coefficient representatives to one prescribed smaller modulus and round. Exact residual shift is bounded by n/2 per coefficient conditional on the full public bank and shared S. Coupling rounded and discrete Gaussian laws gives full-batch planted/comparator gap error <=k0*n*(7*q0/q+(n/2+2)/sigma0). High enough fixed-target noise and auxiliary moduli give a common signed advantage across all auxiliary primes. This closes the average-modulus oracle gap only if MR-v1 and the explicit variable-width AM-MAIN-v3 composition survive new independent review. Target q0 can be any integer; split auxiliary primes remain required. Restricted norm family and standard-hardness gap persist.
