## Primary goal and completion boundary

Establish and critically validate a noncircular, parameter-compatible computational hardness foundation for a noisy low-dimensional ideal source sufficient for coefficient-binary rank-one RLWE recovery hardness. The desired foundation is standard RLWE or Order-LWE in an explicitly verified established-hardness regime, or a precisely specified standard worst-case ideal-lattice problem. State the reduction direction, advantage or success loss, classical/quantum and uniformity model, sample/query budget, noise and dimension changes, and a nonempty effective regime that composes with a binary-secret recovery implication.

The source need not be exactly Base2. A change in ideal distribution, mask law or width, correlations, number of source elements, modulus, or splitting is allowed only with an explicit source experiment, a proved hardness connection and a checked downstream composition. Rank-one coefficient-binary secrets and independent Gaussian target errors remain the desired target. Polynomially many target samples at polynomial q and q/sigma>=n^c for fixed c>0 are preferred. A restricted source/sample or larger-modulus bridge can be valuable partial progress; it does not automatically meet the primary goal. Quantum worst-case reductions may qualify with an explicitly quantum hardness premise and correctly stated target implication; no unlabelled model change is permitted.

**Completion requires the computational connection and a meaningful compatible application.** Generalizing lossiness, optimizing EX-v2, proving a closure property under an unproved source assumption, auditing a previous obstruction, failing to find an attack, or renaming a sparse-CRT-secret RLWE premise does not complete this goal. A scoped obstruction is useful and must inform the next attempt; it is not a general impossibility result or goal achievement. The run can honestly expire unresolved after six hours.
