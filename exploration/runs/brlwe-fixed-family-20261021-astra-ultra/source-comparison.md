Use R=Z[X]/(X^n+1), n a power of two, and a nonzero integral ideal J supplied by an ordinary integer basis, with N(J)<=2^(rho n) for fixed0<=rho<1 and every inherited parameter guard. The actual available radius is r=epsilon_*ell^2/(1024 nK chi Qmax) in J^-1, with an ordinary integer basis of J and N(J)<=2^(rho n). There is no new full FF reduction and hence no new FF-delivered radius. The accepted source classifications below apply directly at this inherited radius, whose guards imply r<1/(4sqrt n). They are classical BDD algorithms, not an inference from an easy wide DGS sampler.

Acceptance-v1.json covers quotient-exponent rounding and the Frobenius prime family; acceptance-v3.json covers repaired symmetry descent and best-standard-subfield contraction. These supply substantive algorithmic classifications, including exponential-norm families and cases with trivial full Galois stabilizer.
