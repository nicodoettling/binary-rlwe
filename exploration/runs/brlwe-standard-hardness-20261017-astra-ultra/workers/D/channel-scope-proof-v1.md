# Proof candidate for D-CS v1

Status: PROVISIONAL. The frozen statement is channel-scope-theorem-only-v1.md. Every probability space is finite except that a target B may itself have been generated by wrapped infinite-support noise; only its finite output law is used. No computational hardness theorem is imported.

For each full multiplier tuple a, the map s -> (a_i s)_i has rank r(a), equal to the number of CRT coordinates with at least one nonzero a_i value. Conditional on a, its uniform-secret image is uniform on a subspace of cardinality q^r(a). The full error vector is independent of a,s by hypothesis, so its addition forms a mixture of translations of this subspace-uniform distribution. Concavity of entropy gives H(b|a)>=r(a)log q. At each CRT coordinate, the all-zero multiplier tuple has probability q^-k; linearity of expectation, without any selected-branch product, yields E r(a)=n(1-q^-k). Thus

    D(P_k||Q_k)=2kn log q-H(P_k)
      <=(k-1+q^-k)n log q.

Write P'=KP_k and Q'=KQ_k. The input P_k is absolutely continuous with respect to full-support Q_k. This property is preserved by a channel, so Q'(y)=0 implies P'(y)=0. On positive-P' atoms the pointwise upper bound on Q' gives log(1/Q'(y))>=log M-alpha. Consequently

    D(P'||Q')=-H(P')+sum_y P'(y)log(1/Q'(y))
      >=log M-alpha-H(P').

The finite log-sum inequality gives KL contraction D(P'||Q')<=D(P_k||Q_k). Combining these proves claim 1's entropy bound. A maximal coupling of P' and B has mismatch probability epsilon. Conditional on the B draw, the other draw can be encoded using the mismatch bit and at most log(M-1) further bits in natural-log units on a mismatch. Equivalently the entropy chain rule gives H(P')-H(B)<=h2(epsilon)+epsilon log M, proving the second inequality. TV(Q',U)<=delta implies Q'(y)-1/M<=delta for every singleton, so Q'(y)<=(1+Mdelta)/M; this supplies the stated alpha.

For claims 2 and 3 the source errors are zero. C has planted probability one, including s=0 and every singular multiplier tuple. At one fixed CRT root, the comparator has independent uniform vectors a,b in F_q^k. There are q^k-1 nonzero a vectors and each has an image line with exactly q possible b vectors; at a=0, only b=0 is in the image. Thus the count of pairs satisfying membership is q(q^k-1)+1 among q^(2k) possibilities. CRT independence under the original uniform comparator yields

    Q_k(C)=[(q(q^k-1)+1)/q^(2k)]^n
      =[q^(1-k)(1-q^-k)+q^(-2k)]^n=gamma.

This product is computed before conditioning on C, not by multiplying posterior estimates. The event can be decided by checking whether the n-by-kn secret multiplication system is consistent, or root by root; zero a vectors are handled explicitly. In the split CRT representation the field-operation cost is O(kn); generic ring coefficient linear algebra is polynomial in kn and log q.

In claim 2 local output coins are fresh and independent of the input. Therefore P'_k=B and Q'_k=gamma B+(1-gamma)U exactly. Homogeneity of signed measures gives TV(Q'_k,U)=gamma TV(B,U). The one-root factor equals

    q^(1-k)[1-q^-k+q^(-k-1)]
      =q^(1-k)[1-(q-1)/q^(k+1)]<q^(1-k).

This proves the gamma bound. If local B and U samplers have whole-output errors xi_B,xi_U, the two endpoints acquire at most xi_B and gamma xi_B+(1-gamma)xi_U respectively; finite implementation claims must additionally account for arithmetic or membership errors if any.

If k>=m+1 then gamma<q^(-(k-1)n)<=q^(-mn)=1/M. For every y, gamma B(y)<=gamma<1/M=U(y). Hence H(y)=[U(y)-gamma B(y)]/(1-gamma)>=0. Its total mass is one by summation. Sampling B on C and H off C therefore gives planted B and comparator gamma B+(1-gamma)H=U, exactly. This existence argument is a valid finite randomized kernel, even where evaluating B's point probabilities or sampling H is computationally hard. No efficient general sampler is inferred.

It checks gamma=(121/625)^2=14641/390625 and every planted noiseless tuple's membership. It checks the rank expectation 48/25 and the exact comparator mixture/TV for the finite-noise Base2_1 target. Claim 3 is additionally checked algebraically by its coordinatewise nonnegativity proof; no second experiment was run.
