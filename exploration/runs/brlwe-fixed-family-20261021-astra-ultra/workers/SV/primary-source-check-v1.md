# Primary source check for cyclotomic arithmetic

Read through the browser on 2026-10-21: [J. S. Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, version 3.08](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf). Proposition 6.2 (printed p.96, PDF p.98) establishes the integer ring, the unique totally ramified prime at the conductor prime, and absence of other ramification for prime-power conductor. Its proof on the next two pages was read. Example 8.18 (printed p.143, PDF p.145) identifies the unramified cyclotomic Frobenius with exponentiation by the rational prime and its order with the residue degree. These are the exact algebraic inputs in SV-BDD-CLASS v1 part6. Set conductor to 2n, distinct from the field degree n.

Also read [Tom Weston, Algebraic Number Theory](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/math5230f08/weston.pdf), Proposition III.3.3 (printed p.70): away from the conductor the residue degree equals multiplicative order modulo the conductor. This corroborates the same identity; no computational theorem is imported.
