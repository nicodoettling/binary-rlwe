# S07 — Directional comparison must retain cubic ring correlations

**Verdict — unresolved hardness bridge; fully written small calculation.** A useful comparison object is the wrapped-noise likelihood field indexed by binary candidates. Its first generic disagreement with an iid design occurs at the third likelihood moment, even when pair moments agree. Preserving only singular values or pairwise directional marginals misses this dependence.

**Reference ledger.** Keep power-of-two degree n, odd polynomial q, rank one, the coefficient basis, iid uniform binary secret, and independent coefficient D_sigma errors reduced using W_sigma,q. First consider one ring sample; m independent samples raise the moments below to their m-th powers. The comparator changes only the public operator to an n×n iid uniform matrix over Z_q. Rotations supply no additional independent samples.

Under the null law, A and B are independent uniform ring elements. Let M_d denote coefficient multiplication by d, and define

\[
L_s=q^n\prod_jW_{\sigma,q}((B-As)_j),\qquad
\phi(u)=\prod_j\mathbb E_{E\sim D_\sigma}e^{2\pi i u_jE/q}.
\]

For d=t−s, Fourier orthogonality gives

\[
\mathbb E L_sL_t=\sum_{M_d^Tu=0}|\phi(u)|^2.
\]

A unit difference therefore gives exactly 1, matching iid. Nonunits can disagree already here. For three distinct candidates s,t,v, put e=v−s. Then

\[
\mathbb E L_sL_tL_v=
\sum_{M_d^Ta+M_e^Tb=0}\phi(a)\phi(b)\phi(a+b).
\]

The iid constraint is instead ad^T+be^T=0. It forces a=b=0: two difference columns of three distinct Boolean vertices have a 2×2 minor ±1, including over composite q. Thus the iid moment equals 1. Ring commutativity supplies a=M_e^Th, b=−M_d^Th. These are correlations between two directional images, first appearing at cubic order after averaging the common B.

Choose h a coefficient vector and set α=E cos(2πE/q)>0. If K counts coordinates where the three bits are not all equal, this summand is exactly α^(2K). Both signs of h give distinct nonzero terms, so the ring moment is at least 1+2α^(2K). For independent binary candidates, K is Binomial(n,3/4), and

\[
\mathbb E\alpha^{2K}=((1+3\alpha^2)/4)^n.
\]

Discarding repeated candidates costs at most 3·2^(−n). Since α≥1−2π²Var(D_sigma)/q², the discrepancy stays bounded below when q²=Ω(n Var(D_sigma)); for growing Gaussian width this is q/sigma=Ω(√n). No claim covers the entire broader small-noise region.

**Typical versus uniform.** Specializing only here to split prime q≡1 mod 2n, any fixed CRT root gives

\[
\Pr[S-T\ne0\text{ and is nonunit}]\ge1/q-2^{-n},
\]

by collision probability ≥1/q at that root. Uniform-over-cube statements cannot discard these directions; even their prior probability need not be cryptographically negligible. Their exploitability still depends on wrapped-noise Fourier weights.

**Baseline comparison — source-verified result and deduction.** Brakerski–Döttling charge a lattice-coset index using spectral control ([2020/1185, pp. 5–6; §5.3](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185.pdf)). Boudgoust–Lapiha already organize pair collisions by difference ideals ([2025/1080, §3.1, Theorem 4](https://hal.science/lirmm-05396888v2/document)). The substantive additional object here is the noise-weighted kernel of two difference operators jointly. Krahmer–Mendelson–Rauhut supply direction-indexed quadratic-chaos bounds, but their real/complex sparse-measurement model changes both arithmetic and vector class ([Theorems 1.1, 1.2, 1.4](https://www.mathc.rwth-aachen.de/~rauhut/files/ChaosRIP.pdf)); applying it directly would change the target.

**Serious objection and missing implication — unresolved.** Raw moments can reflect rare likelihood spikes: in the illustrative zero-noise limit, a unit difference makes the ring cubic moment q^n, supported on A=B=0. Public syzygies also hold under both planted and null laws, so recognizing them is no planted attack. A hardness bridge still needs a source-to-target simulation and control of efficient, data-dependent recovery risk; selected moments provide neither.

**Cheap diagnostic.** Before any iid-universality claim, insert h equal to one coefficient vector into its proposed observable and account for α^(2K), including any truncation that removes it.
