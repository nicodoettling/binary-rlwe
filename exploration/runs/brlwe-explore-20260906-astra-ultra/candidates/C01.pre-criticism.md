# C01 — conditional extraction through a binomial ring quotient

**Status: retained with an unresolved bridge, as an extraction diagnostic.** The conditional extraction statement below follows from an exact calculation and an unconditional source theorem. The natural coefficient-small ideal-plus-error public sampler is efficiently distinguishable. No reduction proving reference-target search or decision hardness is supplied.

## 1. Precise object and statement

Fix constants `K>1` and `tau_0>0`. Let n grow through powers of two, choose a power-of-two divisor `L=Theta((log n)^3)`, and put `d=n/L`. Let q be any prime with `q≡1 mod 2n` and `q≤n^K`. For any `a∈F_q^*` of order `2L`, set

\[
R_q=\mathbb F_q[X]/(X^n+1),\qquad J=(X^d-a),\qquad V=R_q/J.
\]

Let S have independent uniform binary coefficients, and independently sample coefficient `G_j~D_tau`, with any `tau≥tau_0`. Define the auxiliary observation `Y=S+G mod q` coefficientwise. Then

\[
\operatorname{TV}\big(\mathcal L(S\bmod J,Y),
U_V\otimes\mathcal L(Y)\big)=\operatorname{negl}(n). \tag{C01}
\]

Uniform quantifiers: after fixing upper/lower positive constants in `L=Theta(log^3 n)`, for every A>0 there is N such that the distance is at most `n^{-A}` for every n≥N and every qualifying q,a,L,tau. N may depend on A,K,tau_0 and those block-size constants. The statement applies along qualifying prime sequences and does not assert concrete mixing constants or a prime-generation result.

This is a statement about the surrogate Y. It does **not** assert that `S mod J` remains uniform given an actual RLWE transcript. Averages over Y give the joint distance; there is no claim for every fixed leakage value y.

## 2. Mechanism and difference from the earlier framework

The binomial relation reduces the n binary coefficients to d independent blocks:

\[
S\bmod J=\sum_{j<d}X^j\sum_{k<L}a^kS_{j+kd}.
\]

The wrapped Gaussian posterior keeps the bits independent. With `W=W_{tau,q}`,

\[
p_y=\frac{W(y-1)}{W(y)+W(y-1)},\quad
\beta=4\mathbb E[p_Y(1-p_Y)]
=2\sum_y\frac{W(y)W(y-1)}{W(y)+W(y-1)}.
\]

Fourier orthogonality gives exactly

\[
q^d\mathbb E_Y\operatorname{Coll}(S\bmod J\mid Y)=(1+k_L)^d,
\quad
k_L=\sum_{t\ne0}\prod_{k<L}\left[1-\beta\sin^2(\pi ta^k/q)\right].
\]

The factor is the squared characteristic function of an effective Bernoulli law with parameter `(1-sqrt(1-beta))/2`. Its order-2 deficit is `beta/2`. The uniform affine-walk bound in [Breuillard–Varju, Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09053) therefore bounds `k_L` for every qualifying a. This identification changes the collision calculation only; the true secret remains uniform binary.

The full calculation, exact finite bounds, and width dependence are in [C01/calculations.md](C01/calculations.md). In particular `beta≥1-D_tau(0)≥1-D_{tau_0}(0)>0`. The source mixing time is `O(beta^{-1} log(q/delta) log q (log log q)^5)`; taking `delta=n^{-A-1}` makes it `o(log^3 n)`. Tensorizing the scalar bound yields (C01).

The substantive change is a distribution-specific evaluation of a growing-degree quotient under a specified leakage law. It provides `d=Theta(n/log^3 n)` and output length `O(n/log^2 n)` bits. Existing ideal-weighted collision formulas already exist, and the binomial block decomposition itself is not claimed as new. Extraction alone does not overcome the rank-one public-simulation problem.

## 4. Candidate source and its independent definition

The obvious source to test is the following **tailored hidden-binomial-ideal decision problem**. For public n,q,L and perturbation width gamma, sample a uniformly from the roots of `a^L=-1` and keep it hidden. Put `h=X^d-a`, `g=(X^n+1)/h`. Sample independent uniform `P_1,P_2` of degree below d and independent coefficient `F_i~D_gamma^n`. Give

\[
(A_1,A_2)=(gP_1+F_1,\ gP_2+F_2)\pmod q,
\]

and ask to distinguish this pair from two independent uniform elements of R_q. The two samples share a. No binary secret appears in this source task, so it is not a renaming of binary RLWE. Nevertheless, in the small-noise regime below its purported hardness is **refuted** by a polynomial-time test using one sample. It is not a viable assumption.

The independently studied HLBDD source in [Order-LWE, Section 7](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/494.pdf) instead chooses a hidden uniform d-subset T of the n CRT coordinates. Each of its two samples has fresh uniform CRT values on T, zeros elsewhere, and added Gaussian noise in the paper's lattice normalization; the pair is compared to independent uniform ring elements. Its hardness is a nonstandard assumption, not supplied by the paper's worst-case Order-LWE theorem. The exact definition and boundary checks are in [C01/source-checks.md](C01/source-checks.md). No alternative established source satisfying C01's missing requirements has been identified.

## 5. Bridge, distribution obligations, and first missing step

Multiplication by `U∈I=(g)` depends on S only through `Z=S mod J`, since `J I=0`. If U is sampled independently of `(S,Y)`, (C01) permits the statistical replacement

\[
(U,Y,US)\ \approx\ (U,Y,U Z_0),\qquad Z_0\leftarrow U_V,
\]

with the same negligible distance, by data processing. This is a complete, efficiently computable quotient-level implication; reduction modulo h takes O(n) field operations. U may be included in this statement even if a future public transcript would hide it.

If U is invertible on its d active CRT coordinates, `UZ_0` is uniform in I. Without that condition it is uniform only on the smaller image ideal `U R_q`. Uniform U has active noninvertibility probability `1-(1-1/q)^d`, which is not automatically negligible for polynomial q. Thus replacement by an independent uniform element of I needs another argument or a changed sampler.

For a putative mask `A=U+F`, the actual response is

\[
AS+E=US+FS+E.
\]

A natural attempt is to write it as `US+FY+H`, requiring a suitable joint error decomposition `E=F G_tau+H`. The extraction lemma does not provide H, prove the required independence, preserve iid wrapped `D_sigma` errors, or justify the joint law for polynomially many samples. Revealing extra variables correlated with `G_tau` can alter the posterior on which (C01) relies.

The first missing computational step is an independently justified public sampler whose marginal is indistinguishable from uniform and whose responses can be generated with only the permitted quotient information and surrogate leakage at the target noise. The obvious small-F realization fails before the response is formed. No public simulator, secret recovery map, complete reduction direction, or runtime/sample/noise-loss ledger can therefore be claimed. A successful decision-hybrid construction would still need a separate statement connecting it to the reference search task.

Immediate composition with Order-LWE does not repair this gap: C01 handles only a sparse prescribed support family; the old bridge uses the full family and noise-swallowing separations outside the polynomial-width comparison regime. Its active-invertibility step also uses superpolynomial q. These are unmet hypotheses, not an impossibility result for all improvements to that framework.

## 7. Strongest objection and disposition

The matching low-dimensional ideal is a known geometric code in each coefficient block:

\[
(gP)_{j+kd}=a^{L-1-k}P_j.
\]

For a public sample `A=gP+F` with coefficient F in a set E of size s, enumerate `P_j∈F_q` and check all L residual coordinates. Runtime is `O(qn)`; planted acceptance is one if the error is supported in E, while uniform acceptance is at most `q^d(s/q)^n`. One block already gives the bound `q(s/q)^L`. Thus `q/s≥n^c` and `L=Theta(log^3 n)` yield a negligible false-positive probability. A polynomial number of hidden a choices can also be enumerated. Gaussian F is covered by `B=ceil(gamma log n)`, E the centered interval of radius B, and the explicit negligible tail bound in the calculation file.

**Disposition:** retire this public sampler and its source assumption. Retain (C01) as an extraction diagnostic. This attack does not rule out all samplers, general CRT ideals, other observation channels, or binary RLWE.

## 8. One discriminating next lemma

Test the following restricted **wrapped discrete additive-channel obstruction**, for one ring sample. Let `tau_0≤tau≤poly(n)` and `q/sigma≥n^c`. Suppose `A=U+F`, U∈I, and a simulator proposes `US+FY+H`, where H is independent of `(S,G_tau)` conditional on the sampler's coins. If

\[
F G_\tau+H\pmod q
\]

has negligible distance from iid wrapped coefficient `D_sigma`, must the centered coefficients of F lie within `O(sigma log n+1)` except for negligible sampler probability? Formulate closeness jointly with the sampler coins so conditional bad cases cannot be hidden in an average.

A positive answer would extend the decoder objection from an explicitly small-F hypothesis to this natural independent-noise completion template. A counterexample would give a concrete way around the currently strongest obstruction. The statement concerns that additive template only. No extended proof or experiment on it was attempted in this investigation.
