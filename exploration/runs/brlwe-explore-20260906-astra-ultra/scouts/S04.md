# S04 — conditional Fourier extraction through a structured ideal

**Finding.** One mechanism survives: an explicit joint CRT projection of one binary ring element becomes statistically uniform even given independent coefficientwise Gaussian leakage. This supplies an extraction lemma; a rank-one hardness bridge remains **unresolved**.

**Ledger.** Keep rank one, power-of-two degree n, coefficient basis, iid uniform binary S, and the target m independent ring samples with coefficient D_sigma errors. The calculation restricts q to polynomially bounded fully splitting odd primes. Its auxiliary observation is Y=S+G_tau mod q, with independent coefficient G_tau—not the RLWE transcript. It neither changes the target error nor counts rotations as samples. All modular probabilities use W_tau,q.

**Mechanism and precise statement — fully written small calculation using a source-verified result.** For an ideal J, write V=R_q/J, dim(V)=d and v_j=X^j mod J. Given one leakage coordinate y, the posterior bit probability is

p_y=W(y-1)/(W(y)+W(y-1)); set beta=4 E[p_Y(1-p_Y)].

Independence and Fourier orthogonality give exactly

q^d E_Y Coll(S mod J | Y)
= sum_{ell in V*} product_{j<n}[1-beta sin²(pi ell(v_j)/q)].

The factor equals the squared Fourier transform of Bernoulli(p_beta), where p_beta=(1-sqrt(1-beta))/2. Thus a conditional collision calculation becomes an ordinary biased-Bernoulli mixing calculation; the actual secret remains uniform binary.

Choose L|n, d=n/L, r of order 2n, a=r^d, and J=(X^d-a). Here ord(a)=2L and X^d-a divides X^n+1. Reduction separates d independent blocks of L bits. If k_L is the nontrivial Fourier sum for one block, conditional statistical distance is at most

(1/2) sqrt((1+k_L)^d-1).

[Breuillard–Varjú, Section 2, Theorem 5 and Lemma 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09053) give k_L<=delta² when ord(a)>=C log q logloglog q and

L >= (2C/beta) log(q/delta) log q (loglog q)^5.

Consequently beta>=beta_0>0 and L a power of two of size Theta(log³ n) give negligible conditional distance for polynomial q: take delta=n^{-A} for arbitrary fixed A. The quotient degree is Theta(n/log³ n), comfortably inside the conditional collision-entropy budget. Lemma 6 controls orbit concentration through low-order exceptions; sharper “most multipliers” results are unnecessary here.

**First difference from existing approaches.** [2025/1080, Theorems 4–5 and Corollaries 6–7](../sources/2025-1080.txt) already supply ideal-weighted collision identities and short-difference universality. The useful change is source-specific conditional extraction for this prescribed quotient. The block decomposition itself is old: [Lyubashevsky–Seiler, Section 3.2, equations (12)–(13)](https://crypto.ethz.ch/publications/files/LyuSei17.pdf). Neither merely strengthens the scalar projection bound of 2020/119, Theorem 4.1, or pays the lattice-coset index in 2020/1185, Theorem 6.9.

**Adversarial checks.** Feasible two splitting has degree n/2 factors; irreducibility over odd primes is impossible for n>=4. At q>n, the standard norm bound makes every nonzero binary difference a unit, so each prime projection is injective and cannot compress binary information. Conversely q=3 admits D=X^{n/2}+X^{n/4}-1. Its prime quotient gives independent four-bit blocks uniform on eight of nine residues: TV=1-(8/9)^{n/4}, despite d log₂q<0.793n. This **refutes** unrestricted entropy-only extraction, but fixed q does not meet the target growing noise separation. Exact enumeration confirmed n=4,8. At n=8,q=17, scalar TV=1/272; larger-quotient nonflatness in the experiment exceeds the entropy budget and is only a boundary check.

**Missing implication and serious objection.** A public nonunit multiplier reveals its kernel; units preserve conditional collision. No pseudorandom rank-one public simulator spending only J-information is supplied. Posterior independence also fails for the actual mixed transcript. The 2020 ring entropy obstruction therefore remains.
