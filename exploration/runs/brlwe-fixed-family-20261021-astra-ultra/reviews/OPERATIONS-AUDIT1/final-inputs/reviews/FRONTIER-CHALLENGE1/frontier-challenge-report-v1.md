## Additional precision-tuning escape / correction to the initial list

The initial F1 wording treated a single exact or negligible-error channel as the natural sufficient target. That is stronger than the task strictly requires. The reduction may depend polynomially on k and 1/epsilon while the **target family stays fixed**. Therefore a uniformly constructible family of channels K_eta with per-input full-law error eta and cost polynomial in 1/eta (or log(1/eta)) can be sufficient when eta is chosen <=epsilon divided by a charged polynomial in the total number of uses. Its auxiliary source parameters may also depend on those resources. A single resource-independent negligible-error kernel is sufficient, but not necessary.

This is an additional explicit open interface F1-PRECISION, not a new experiment: specify how eta enters the channel/table construction, prove both conditional planted and comparator laws, and derive the end-to-end error/cost with the exact number of uses. The new kernel must remove the fixed **structural** mismatch floor; merely improving the sampler precision does not remove MR's rounding or completion mismatch. Expected future effort: 2–4 hours to write and audit one concrete channel; a small exact test only after its model is frozen. This possibility is not excluded by the present finite kernels or affine lower bounds.
