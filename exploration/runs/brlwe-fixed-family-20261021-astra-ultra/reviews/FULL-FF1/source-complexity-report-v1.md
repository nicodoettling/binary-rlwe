# FULL-FF1 source, encoding and complexity report

Exact scope: CRT-PROJ-v1 and CERT-EXTRACT-v1, with the unchanged DD1-EXTRACT-v1 and DD2-AM1-SNF-v1 local interfaces. The new claims do not inherit historical acceptance automatically. Input hashes and exact accepted-import comparisons are recorded in allowed-inputs-v1.json.

## Endpoints and quantifiers

The certificate extractor receives a physical batch of K=r k(Ncal+Ntest) ordered ring pairs in R_q^2. The exact target uses one uniform coefficient-binary S for the whole batch, uniform public A and independent exact coefficient discrete-Gaussian errors. D receives k ordered transformed pairs, fixed identical metadata and fresh private coins. Comparator responses are independent uniform elements with the same public multiplier law. Intermediate prefix and candidate calls are transformed laws, not assertions that every call is itself drawn from the exact target promise. All-input bounded D code is needed to charge these calls.

The output is FAIL or r distinct root/value pairs. Their total size is O(r log q); their injectivity among binary vectors does not provide a polynomial inverse. The proof's exhaustive inverse is only an information-theoretic upper-bound device. Selecting roots from the observation does not let a reviewer move a maximum outside the observation average. Here the stronger property holds for every possible output tuple, so adaptive choice does not weaken injectivity.

The finite claim must supply encoded rational epsilon and delta (and explicitly charge any numerical representation it accepts), together with a bounded code/evaluation model. V1's omission is MODEL-1; DD1's rational-input premise is not silently inherited. Fresh independent D tapes are also load-bearing for iid concentration. Sigma defines the exact error-law promise; the extractor itself is not tasked with exact Gaussian sampling. A rational sigma or a precisely stated nonalgorithmic promise suffices, and sigma=n^2 is integral in the corollary. The guard k*tau<=epsilon/4 can remain a mathematical promise; computing a transcendental bound for tau is not part of the extractor unless expressly requested.

The strict integer r is effectively computed by comparing q^(2r) with n^n. Log2 ceilings for positive rationals use integer comparisons. Once epsilon/delta are rational, empirical thresholds and distances are exact rational comparisons; no unbounded real-equality test is required.

## Costs and fixed family

The stated physical raw count K is correct. Reuse within calibration/test banks increases calls but not raw samples. At each stage the complement has m<=n roots. All calibration prefix masks consume k Ncal m(m+1)/2 field values. For each of q candidate values, its selected prefix uses at most m-1 masks plus one p, at most n draws per physical sample. Summing stages gives the displayed Tfield bound. The call bound r((n+1)Ncal+qNtest) also holds. With b=ceil(log2 q), at most B Tfield b fresh fair bits are used for the field samplers, in addition to the explicitly charged D tapes.

All ring data can be encoded by n canonical residues in [0,q-1]. Root enumeration may test all q field elements; CRT idempotents follow by polynomial interpolation or linear algebra. These are polynomial in numeric q,n, with modular arithmetic bit costs charged. Enumeration is not polynomial merely in log q. The finite bounds allow arbitrary D evaluation cost; asymptotic PPT follows only when its supplied all-input bound and all required encoded inputs are polynomially bounded. Reductions may depend on the adversary's polynomial k and inverse-advantage exponents while the chosen q(n),sigma(n) remain fixed.

Effective fixed q(n) exists from the exact accepted DD2-PRIMES-v1 construction: set J=R, M=1 and Qmin=n^4. Its output depends only on n, lies in 1 mod2n, and has numeric size O(n^24) with an absolute effective constant, absorbable into a larger fixed exponent B. Trial enumeration terminates in a fixed polynomial without requiring the numerical constant as advice. No source ideal is used to select this target family. The underlying imported result is Xylouris, Acta Arithmetica150(2011), Theorem1.1, printed p.66: least prime in a coprime progression is bounded by an effective constant times the modulus to power5.18. The publisher's primary PDF was opened and its theorem statement plus introductory effective-constant formulation inspected: https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/83078 . DD2 uses the weaker exponent6. This source establishes a prime bound, not an RLWE hardness assumption or practical search constant.

The new flattening bound is exp(-Omega(n^3)) independent of the adversary's fixed polynomial exponents. Thus k*tau<=epsilon/4 eventually for every polynomial k and inverse-polynomial epsilon, using one fixed target q(n),sigma(n)=n^2. This is a valid advance for a decision-to-certificate auxiliary. It does not remove the independent source-bridge constraints.

## Source and hardness boundaries

No source BDD input appears in either new theorem. Accordingly neither changes the exact BDD radius previously delivered by FIXED-MAIN-v2, proves a new radius, nor creates an independently established hardness assumption. The SV ordinary-basis classifications remain separate author claims, not dependencies or automatically accepted consequences of this review.

A-FRAC requires sigma>=2ts sqrt(Kn)L for its stacked physical source matrix. The new K includes r stages and both calibration/test banks. It cannot be replaced by k or by the D-call count, and its dependence on arbitrary adversary exponents is still unresolved. A wide classically simulable DGS oracle gives neither a BDD decoder nor a reverse reduction; no DGS comparison is used here. Integral-map and short-complementary-multiplier obstructions from RH remain in their accepted scopes.

A second source-composition boundary is coprimality. A fixed polynomial-value split prime q(n) cannot be coprime to N(J) for every eligible integral ideal: J=(q(n),X-alpha) has index q(n)<=2^(rho n) eventually. The certificate theorem does not require an ideal or coprimality, so this is not a defect in it. A future direct same-q source bridge importing gcd(q,N(J))=1 must handle such instances or state the restriction. These polynomial-characteristic prime examples are covered by the separate SV easy-family construction; that fact alone does not settle arbitrary q-primary/composite ideals.

No independently established assumption is mapped effectively to the new source at exact resources and endpoints. Therefore source hardness remains unestablished even if the auxiliary certificate statements pass all reviews. All-input implementation, fixed-family quantifiers, source diagnosis and source hardness remain separate statuses.
