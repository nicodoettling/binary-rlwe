# FF3 selector compression after the first seal

Author-provisional; additive to author-phase1-seal-v1.json. The original positive FV9 certificate is unchanged.

FV10 tested three explicit samplers using the four frozen FV9 ring-noise H vertices. For an edge (u,u+X^i), let z0,z1 be zero counts of B-Au and B-A(u+X^i). The models allowed: uniform edge with branch-vertex weights depending only on(z0,z1); nonuniform edge/vertex weights depending on(z0,z1) with exact per-input total normalization; and dependence on(i,z0,z1). Every model includes all four fixed-secret endpoint laws and the uniform comparator. All three are infeasible in this exact n2,Q3,q2 rational model. The rational Farkas witnesses satisfy A^T z>=0 and b^T z<0; their full fractions, hashes and code are in experiments/ff3-local-selector-fv10-* and local-selector-*-witness.json. The job completed in0.58s with69632KiB peak RSS, exit0, and no remaining matching process.

New regression FF3-LOCAL-SELECTOR-FAIL-1: reject any claim that the FV9 feasible selector can be replaced by one of these local tables. This does not exclude observing additional public information, such as the zero count of A, nor a different ring H. It is recorded before testing those alternatives.

The next motivated compression includes the public zero count of A, in addition to the two edge residual zero counts. For centered ternary coefficient vectors, zero count determines squared norm. The added statistic records the norm of the difference of the two residuals and can distinguish their geometric overlap. Such a table still has polynomially many classes in n; a uniform edge is efficiently sampled by selecting i and the remaining n-1 candidate bits. If nonnegative local edge weights have exact total E on every input and maximum local sum M, rejection from a uniform edge and accepting with local_sum/M gives the desired selector in expected M trials. No scalable existence claim follows from n2 success; one must prove both the tables and their bounds for the full parameter family.
