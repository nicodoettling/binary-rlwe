**HLBDD / Order-LWE.** The checked full 2018/494 revision, 2019-09-04, Section 7, uses two independently noised uniform ideal points sharing its specified hidden support. The initialized oracle and Lemma 7.7 fix the intended shared-ideal interpretation; notation ambiguities and exact normalization are recorded in the source ledger. HLBDD is separately assumed, not proved from worst-case Order-LWE. It has neither A2's active-U law nor its F-dependent H kernel. Its entropic construction additionally uses ordinary RLWE, swallowing ratios and a superpolynomial-q invertibility argument. It therefore supplies neither exact A2 source hardness nor the current polynomial-q target through a silent import.

**DSPR / entropic Ring-LWE.** The primary 2020/1185 source has a genuine common-denominator joint-tuple hybrid under DSPR and Hermite-form RLWE. Their actual task and sampling laws remain attached to their own results.

F03/R02 explicitly enumerate grouped supersupports before calling the known-support routine. The resulting exp(O_c(d)) route has a stated sufficient gap and uniform false-positive bound. The imported solver is probabilistic; retain its p_alg success in the finite advantage. This is an attack connection, not a converse hardness reduction. R02's uniqueness and fixed-character bounds are separate deductions awaiting criticism, neither evidence of source hardness.

**Extraction sources.** D01 now uses the unconditional Section 5 Proposition 23 of Breuillard–Varju arXiv:1810.13360v2, together with Voutier Corollary 2, to derive full joint extraction at the explicitly smaller d. The scalar affine-walk theorem alone would not supply joint CRT extraction. The ring-LHL manuscript already contains ideal-weighted collisions and a leakage-conditioned identity, but its quoted corollaries do not directly supply this selector experiment. These precise source distinctions support a theorem import, not a novelty assertion.

**Correlated-noise precedent.** The different four-pair determinant attack in 2020/415 was checked. Its reciprocal denominators and source law differ, so it is not represented as an attack on A2. R02's test of fixed additive characters retains H conditional F and only excludes that bounded attack class. A2's first marginal is statistically far from uniform; negligible individual character bias does not imply computational pseudorandomness.

**Multiple samples.** D03's common-K tuple and full stacked H_seq define another explicit assumption within A's sample-extension analysis. It is not a product of the one-pair source, and its H marginals are not asserted to be one-sample H_seq. No matching primary source establishes its pseudorandomness. Existing extra-RLWE or subset-sum expansion methods change assumptions, error laws, widths or sample interfaces.

The bounded targeted search found no matching independent reduction for the precise A2 or common-K sources. This is a recorded search outcome, not a universal nonexistence or novelty claim. Laws from these separate sources cannot be assembled into one construction.
