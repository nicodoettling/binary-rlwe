# New scoped negative controls, recorded before any repair

No frozen candidate has been rejected and no theorem repair is proposed. These controls reject nearby stronger statements and must remain binding.

**FRONTIER1-NONGAUSS-TV.** Take n=4, Z=0 and Delta=0 with probability 1-10^-6, Delta=+1000 e_0 and -1000 e_0 each with probability 1/(2*10^6). Then C=e_0 e_0^T has trace 1. Let W_*=0. For every nonzero binary s, W_s is nonzero exactly on the rare two atoms; at s=0 it is always zero. Retained-secret TV is therefore (15/16)*10^-6=3/3200000. All conditional covariance norms are at most 4, so L=16 is valid. A Gaussian-style lower bound would exceed 1/8192, since exp(-1/2)>1/2. Thus merely imposing finite second moments does not extend GAUSS-AVG-v1. The exact Gaussian assumption excludes this control. WEIGHT-COV-v1 supplies only an equality obstruction and survives it. Reproduced in controls-v1.py.

**FRONTIER1-SECRET-ADVICE.** Let n=4, S uniform binary, Delta uniform on {+e_0,-e_0}, and Z=M_S Delta. Then W_S=0 identically, with nonzero Delta covariance. The conditional law of (Delta,Z) varies with S, even when S is restricted to the weight-2 slice. Hence arbitrary secret advice invalidates the independence/common-law premise. Weight advice alone does not permit these within-slice changes. Thirty-two exact joint atoms were checked. The frozen Gaussian and weight claims explicitly exclude this construction.

**FRONTIER1-REUSED-ML.** Let P=(9/10,1/10), Q=(1/10,9/10). Their affinity is exactly 3/5. Ten iid observations support the usual affinity power bound, checked by exhaustive rational enumeration for every K=1,...,10 with deterministic ties. Ten copies of one observation instead retain conditional error 1/10, which exceeds (3/5)^10=59049/9765625. This rejects powering under repeated data or under the unconditioned mixture over a retained common secret. FRAC-INFO-v1 uses fresh observations conditionally on fixed S, and survives.

**FRONTIER1-ZERO-A.** In n=2,q=3, let A=0 with probability 1/2 and each of the eight other multipliers have probability 1/16; let independent coefficient error masses be (3/5,1/5,1/5). Each conditional law has full support and every pair of binary-secret laws has TV 11/50, but conditional on A=0 the secret posterior is uniform for every B. Pairwise unconditional separation therefore must not become a pointwise posterior separation claim. FRAC-INFO-v1 never makes that conversion.

**FRONTIER1-RAMIFIED-SQUARE.** In K=Q(zeta_8), put L=Q(sqrt(2)) and J=(sqrt(2)), represented by f=X-X^3. Then [O_L:J intersect O_L]=2 and [R_4:J]=4, so the local comparison d_m<=d_L^2 can be equality at a ramified prime. The exact four-dimensional residue calculation is in controls-v1.py. An unramified restriction is unnecessary for norm extension; replacing the square by the first power would be false. For J=(1-zeta_8), the same contraction has d_L=2 but d_m=2<4 (hand valuation calculation: the relative ramification index is 2). These are controls on the local comparison, not claims of sharpness for the global minima.

**FRONTIER1-CHARACTERISTIC-COUNT.** In degree 16 take two distinct prime ideals over the completely split rational prime 97, each to exponent one. Then e=97, B=2 log2(97), and N(J)=97^2=9409<2^(0.9*16). Counting only one rational characteristic would incorrectly imply B<=log2(e). The correct bound is B<=t log2(e) with t the number of supported prime ideals per characteristic. The frozen source-support theorem states exactly that restriction. This is a hand exact arithmetic witness; no factoring job was needed.
