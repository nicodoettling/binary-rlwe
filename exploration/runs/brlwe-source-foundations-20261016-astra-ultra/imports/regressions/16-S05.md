# S05 — Arithmetic exceptional fibers and their perturbations

**Explicit family; unresolved.** Let R_q=F_q[X]/(X^n+1), with n a power of two and q odd prime. Choose an ideal I of F_q-dimension ρ; sample A_i=U_i+F_i, where U_i is uniform in I and F_i has independent integer coefficients uniform on [-H,H], reduced modulo q. All samples share I. The structural question is whether efficiently sampled ideals can give substantial *average* Boolean ambiguity while these perturbed multipliers are computationally indistinguishable from uniform at transcript noise σ≤q/n^c. Exact exceptional multipliers are publicly recognizable; computational hiding after perturbation is an unproved bridge.

**Fully written small calculation.** Multiplication by A has field fibers of size q^{deg gcd(A,X^n+1)}, when nonempty. This does not count Boolean points. For q≡1 mod4, choose u²=-1 and k=n/2. If A=(X^k-u)T with T a unit, its kernel consists of (P,Q)=(uQ,Q), writing S=P+X^kQ. A binary difference has coefficients in {-1,0,1}; since u∉{0,±1}, this kernel contains no nonzero binary difference. Thus its q^{n/2}-point field fibers contain at most one Boolean point. Neither degree-k factor needs to be irreducible. Conversely, a rank-one CRT ideal when q≡1 mod2n gives mean Boolean fiber size 2^n/q over the q possible outputs, including empty fibers; this is not the random-secret mean. Large field dimension alone is therefore a **refuted** Boolean-lossiness heuristic.

**Source-verified mechanism; deductions distinguished.** The norm is the determinant of multiplication and commutes with reduction ([Stacks, Lemma 31.18.6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BD2)). For nonzero ternary δ of weight w annihilating I, my calculation gives

\[
q^\rho\mid\operatorname{Res}(X^n+1,\delta),\qquad
0<|\operatorname{Res}(X^n+1,\delta)|\le w^{n/2}.
\]

The first statement follows from modular nullity; the second from irreducibility over Q, Parseval, and AM–GM. If A is ε-close to uniform statistically, support counting requires (2H+1)^n≥(1-ε)q^{n-ρ}. For ε<1/2, H≥1, and each unwrapped coordinate of Fδ has variance at least (2/27)(1-ε)^{2/n}q². This obstructs preserving a fixed collision at small noise by statistical mixing. It does **not** exclude computational hiding or adaptively selected collisions, and variance is not a wrapped-noise tail bound.

**Experiment.** [Delsarte–Goethals–Seidel, §4 and Corollary 7.2](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1971-091-x) construct negacyclic conference polynomials c satisfying cc*=q at degree n=q+1. My exact n=8,q=7 diagnostic found a rank-four example. A full-support generator of I=Ann(c) has 224 singleton and sixteen double Boolean fibers: only 32 of 256 secrets collide, although every nonempty field fiber has 2401 points. Public A-distance for H=1,2,3 is respectively 0.2512, 0.008251,0; fixed-collision RMS is 2.16,3.74,5.29. Code and hand checks are in `S05/`; enumeration took 0.13 seconds. These finite parameters establish no growing family: requiring q+1 to be a power of two restricts prime q to Mersenne primes, whose infinitude is not assumed.

**Baseline comparison and serious objection.** Ideal-conditioned difference counting already appears explicitly in [Boudgoust–Lapiha, Theorem 4](https://hal.science/lirmm-05396888v2/document); the arithmetic representation itself is not new. [Brakerski–Döttling, pp.2–6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185) replace direct low-rank lossiness with short-basis lossiness and identify a coset-entropy obstacle for binary secrets. The family above bypasses neither issue. A dense ternary relation can affect very few uniform secrets; finding one is insufficient. There is also a concrete decoding objection: fully split ideals with consecutive CRT support become generalized Reed–Solomon codes. With constant box perturbation, [Guruswami–Sudan decoding](https://people.csail.mit.edu/madhu/papers/1998/venkat-journ.pdf) distinguishes sufficiently low-rate instances; the coefficient-to-code translation is my deduction, recorded in `S05/`. Any remaining lead must justify its choice of CRT support. The cheapest next diagnostic is to count the entire Boolean-fiber histogram for admissible ideals before investigating pseudorandomness, then test quotient distributions and *wrapped* collision displacements after perturbation.
