# S01 — planted posterior and an exact refresh channel

Two mechanisms were examined. The useful object is a noise-weighted annihilator enumerator; no hardness transfer is established.

**Fully written small calculation.** Set \(p(e)=\prod_jW_{\sigma,q}(e_j)\), \(L_s=\prod_iq^np(B_i-A_is)\), and \(Z=2^{-n}\sum_sL_s\). The exact Boolean-cube posterior is
\[
 \pi(s\mid A,B)=\frac{\prod_{i,j}W_{\sigma,q}((B_i-A_is)_j)}
 {\sum_{t\in\{0,1\}^n}\prod_{i,j}W_{\sigma,q}((B_i-A_it)_j)}.
\]
Its Hamiltonian is the negative logarithm of the numerator, not an unwrapped quadratic. A conditional free energy is
\(\Phi_h=n^{-1}\mathbb E_{\rm pl}\log[2^{-n}\sum_{d_H(t,S)=h}L_t]\).
Posterior replicas and the planted secret are conditionally exchangeable, so Nishimori identities remain valid despite ring correlations. This generic identity is source-verified in [Barbier et al., Appendix A.1, Proposition 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.03395#page=68); their free-energy theorem assumes independent matrix entries and does not transfer automatically.

**Mechanism 1: planting comparison.** For the specified uniform-pairs null \(Q\), \(dP_{\rm pl}/dQ=Z\). Finite Fourier orthogonality gives
\[
 \mathbb E_QZ^2=\mathbb E_{s,t}K(s-t)^m,\qquad
 K(\Delta)=\sum_{v\in\ker M_\Delta^T}\prod_j|\widehat W(v_j)|^2,
\]
where \(M_\Delta\) is negacyclic multiplication and
\(\widehat W(k)=\sum_rW(r)e^{2\pi ikr/q}\).
Only different ring samples supply independence. Units give \(K=1\); identical replicas contribute \(2^{-n}(q\sum_rW(r)^2)^{mn}\). This is an annealed calculation, not a typical-instance recovery theorem.

**Refuted:** statistical quiet planting toward this null throughout the stated polynomial small-noise region. With \(N=mn\ge n\) and \(T=\lfloor\sigma\sqrt{\log(2Nn^2)/\pi}\rfloor\), a union over binary secrets and a discrete-Gaussian tail bound yield
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(P_{\rm pl},Q)\ge1-n^{-2}-(2/3+o(1))^n.
\]
The separating event asks whether some binary secret leaves every residual inside the modular box \([-T,T]^N\); evaluating it can take exponential time. Divergent second moments alone would not prove this separation. The comparison inspired by [Zdeborová–Krzakala, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.4185#page=11) therefore fails statistically without contradicting computational pseudorandomness.

**Mechanism 2: exact channel interpolation.** Independently add coefficient noise
\(\mathcal C_u=e^{-u}\delta_0+(1-e^{-u})U_q\).
This efficiently samplable degradation preserves the secret and public ring distribution and produces
\(W_u=e^{-u}W+(1-e^{-u})U_q\), with
\[
 K_u(\Delta)=\sum_{v\in\ker M_\Delta^T}
 e^{-2u\operatorname{wt}(v)}\prod_j|\widehat W(v_j)|^2.
\]
Exact entropy dissipation for the coordinate-refresh generator is written in the supporting calculations; it makes no replica-symmetry assumption.

**Unresolved, falsifiable question:** for \(n=2^k,q=n^2+1,\sigma=n,m=1\), is \(\mathbb E_{s,t}[\mathbf1_{s\ne t}(K_u(s-t)-1)]=o(1)\) when \(e^{-2u}=1/[2(q\sum W^2-1)]\)? The identical-replica term then vanishes exponentially. An independent-uniform-row comparator has no nonzero-difference contribution. A cheap next diagnostic is to enumerate unit/nonunit frequencies by overlap at \(n=8,q=17\), then evaluate one-dimensional annihilator kernels with \(\sigma=1\), using the true pair-difference probabilities. Already \(X^3-X-1\) is a nonunit while \(X^2-X-1\) is a unit, despite equal Hamming weight and signed counts. Thus scalar overlap does not determine the kernel.

**Baseline comparison and serious objection.** The noiseless kernel is the ideal-index contribution in [Boudgoust–Lapiha, Theorem 4, pp.17–18](https://hal.science/lirmm-05396888v2/document); the present calculation adds the actual noise weights, not a new ideal mechanism. It neither reduces the lattice-coset leakage nor satisfies the binary entropy deficit in [Brakerski–Döttling, p.6 and Theorem 6.9](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185). Refreshing changes the error law, and its reduction direction only carries presumed original hardness toward the contaminated family. A reverse, solution-preserving simulation from a hard source remains missing; free-energy agreement would not supply it.

Distribution ledger: original (n,q,m), rank one, coefficient basis and binary prior are preserved until the explicitly marked refresh/comparator changes. The finite toy is not an asymptotic family. No experiments, algorithm-model lower bounds, or cryptographic hardness claims were produced. Derivations and source/access records are in `S01/prebarrier-notes.md` and `S01/sources.md`.
