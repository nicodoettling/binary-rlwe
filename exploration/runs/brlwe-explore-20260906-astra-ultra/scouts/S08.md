# S08 — public affine orbit sampling and its exact reverse barrier

**Status: fully written small calculations; hardness implication unresolved.** Independent development ran 09:52:36–10:03:00 UTC before baseline access. Two mechanisms follow. Neither proves binary-RLWE hardness. Detailed proofs, restrictions, and source/access records are in `scouts/S08/development.md` and `scouts/S08/sources.md`.

**1. Exact affine binary-orbit sampler.** Put $H=1+X+\cdots+X^{n-1}$. Choose independently uniform $j\bmod 2n$ and odd $k\bmod 2n$, set $u=X^j$, $\phi_k(f)=f(X^k)$, and $t=(H-u\phi_k(H))/2$, computed over the integers. The coefficient map $u\phi_k$ is a signed permutation, so $t$ has binary coefficients. Execute publicly, for every input sample,

\[
 A_i'=u^{-1}\phi_k(A_i),\qquad
 B_i'=\phi_k(B_i)+A_i't.
\]

The coupled secret and error are

\[
 S'=u\phi_k(S)+t,\qquad E_i'=\phi_k(E_i).
\]

Expansion verifies the sample equation. Conditional on the public coins, the $A_i'$ remain independent uniform ring elements; coefficient sign/permutation invariance gives independent $D_\sigma$ errors. These distributions do not depend on the coins, establishing the full joint law after averaging. Degree, modulus, rank one, coefficient basis, width, and $m$ ring samples are unchanged. The public inverse recovers $S$ from $S'$.

On centered secrets $V=2S-H$, this is $V\mapsto u\phi_k(V)$. The acting group has order $2n^2$. One step samples exactly uniformly within the starting secret's orbit; a prior $\mu$ becomes its orbit symmetrization. Every orbit-uniform measure is stationary. Starting from a fixed secret, distance from uniform binary remains at least $\max\{0,1-2n^2/2^n\}$, at every time. There is no global mixing. Repeated transformed transcripts are correlated, never fresh samples.

A restricted classification explains this small orbit. For samplewise affine transformations with a fixed signed-permutation error map $Q$, an affine binary-cube bijection has signed-permutation linear part $P$. Compatibility requires $QM(a)P^{-1}$ to remain a ring multiplication matrix for every $a$. At $a=1$, $QP^{-1}$ must be multiplication by a signed monomial. Consequently $Q$ normalizes the negacyclic algebra, forcing $Q=X^h\phi_k$, and $P$ belongs to the same family. Arbitrary coordinate flips available for scalar LWE therefore do not transfer within this class.

**2. Exact public translation, and why adaptive reversal fails.** Publicly choose $t\in R_q$, retain $A_i$, and replace $B_i$ by $B_i+A_it$. Then $S'=S+t$, with unchanged errors. Fresh uniform $t$ gives a uniform ring secret in one step. This changes only the secret prior and gives the easy binary-search-to-uniform-search direction.

Allow any transcript-dependent transition $K(t\mid A,B)$. Exact output prior $\pi$, with the same full sample channel, requires

\[
 \sum_t\mu(s'-t)K(t\mid A,B'-At)=\pi(s').
\]

Indeed, the common residual likelihood
$\prod_{i,r}W_{\sigma,q}((B_i'-A_is')_r)>0$
cancels. For uniform input $\mu$, the left side is independent of $s'$, forcing $\pi$ uniform. Thus transcript-adaptive translation cannot exactly reverse the prior change. More generally, public selection among channel symmetries preserves an invariant prior whenever the output must retain that same joint channel.

**Comparison and next test.** These moves differ substantively from subset-sum sample rerandomization by retaining the original Gaussian width and finite sample count; BD20 explicitly changes to a distribution of error distributions ([§3.1, Lemma 3.5](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185)). They supply no replacement for its lattice-coset entropy cost ([p.6, Theorem 6.9](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1185)), nor ordinary lossiness's freely chosen projection ([§1.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/119)). No originality claim is made for exploiting ring symmetries.

The missing hardness step is a publicly executable, recoverable move beyond this restricted family with a proved target joint law. Serious objection: the positivity argument is **exact only**; tiny Gaussian tails prevent any claimed exclusion of negligible statistical approximation. Nonlinear, sample-consuming, or noise-changing kernels lie outside the proved restriction.
